Academic expression and data protection

GDPR Article 85 and Swedish Data Protection Act (2018:218), Chapter 1, Section 7 provide a framework for academic-expression exceptions. Their application depends on the purpose of the processing. An author name remains personal data; including it does not waive applicable rights or make all AIRR processing exempt. See the privacy notice.

Submissions remain private during review. Publication requires a separate permission for the accepted version. If published, the chosen author credit and information inside that PDF will be publicly accessible and may be copied or indexed elsewhere.
