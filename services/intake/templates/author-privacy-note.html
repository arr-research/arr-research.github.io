More about author credit and privacy

Check the PDF's pages and document properties. Omit identity numbers and unnecessary sensitive information. Include other people's details only when necessary and lawful, with authority for co-author credit. AIRR does not automatically remove information from a PDF, and leaving the author field blank does not remove names inside it.

GDPR Article 85 and Swedish Data Protection Act (2018:218), Chapter 1, Section 7 provide a framework for academic-expression exceptions. Their application depends on the purpose of the processing. An author name remains personal data; including it does not waive applicable rights or make all AIRR processing exempt. See the privacy notice.

If you later authorize publication, the chosen author credit and information inside that PDF will be publicly accessible and may be copied or indexed elsewhere.
