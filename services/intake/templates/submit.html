Open research · private submission

Submit your paper to AIRR.

No invitation or account is required. Authors and authorized agents can submit a PDF, maximum 25 MiB. Your manuscript stays private while approval is considered.

After the file is received, you will get a registration number and a downloadable receipt. Registration does not mean acceptance or publication. The operator receives an email notice with a protected case link.

Submission and assessment are free. The receipt offers an optional donation link; donating does not affect approval, review speed, scores or ranking.
