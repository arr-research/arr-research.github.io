1. Introduction
Fix a ϐinite rank‑one POVM seed 𝐹= {(𝑤𝑖, 𝑥𝑖)}𝑁
𝑖=1 in ℂ𝑑and rotate it by every unitary. Under the
homogeneous‑response ansatz, normalisation on this orbit constrains a scalar ray response 𝑓on
ℂℙ𝑑−1, and the strength of that constraint on the harmonic sector of degree 𝑘is a number 𝑔𝑘(𝐹) (the
record [Rec], Theorem 3.1). The optimal 𝐿2 inverse constant off the afϐine trace‑one quadratic sector
is 𝛾(𝐹)−1/2 with 𝛾(𝐹) = inf𝑘≥2 𝑔𝑘(𝐹) ([Rec], Deϐinition 3.2 and Theorem 3.3). The design question of
[Rec] is: among outcome‑simple equal‑trace seeds, which seed has the largest gap Γ𝑑= sup𝐹𝛾(𝐹)? Its
Theorem 5.2 answers this for 𝑑≥5: Γ𝑑= 𝑑−6/(𝑑+ 1), attained only by an orthonormal basis (ONB).
The proof compares the ONB with the 𝑁= 𝑑+ 1 regular simplex at degree 𝑘= 3 and kills 𝑁≥𝑑+ 2
by the ceiling 𝛾≤𝑑2/𝑁; the degree‑three comparison reverses sign in 𝑑= 3 and 𝑑= 4 ([Rec], Section
7.1), and the record explicitly makes no claim there.
This paper settles 𝑑= 4 and narrows 𝑑= 3.

7. Open questions
1. The value of Γ3. Prove Γ3 = 𝛾𝐵(Section 5.5), or ϐind a better upper bound than 9/5.
