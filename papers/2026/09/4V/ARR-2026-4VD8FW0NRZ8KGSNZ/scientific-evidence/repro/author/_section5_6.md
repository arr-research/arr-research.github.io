### 5.3 Exact lower-bound certificate (PROVED-COMPUTER-ASSISTED, exact integer arithmetic)
`s5_rationalise_and_certify.py` builds from the 40-digit point B an EXACT frame: n_1, n_2, n_3 rational unit vectors (stereographic rational points with denominator 10^8), w rational ⊥ s = n_1+n_2+n_3, n_{4,5} = −s/2 ± √q w with q = (1 − |s|²/4)/|w|² rational (spec file `s6_refined_B_exact_den100000000.json`; unit norms and Σn_i = 0 are asserted exactly in Fractions). `s4int_d3_exact_certificate.py` evaluates g_k, 2 ≤ k ≤ 600, exactly in Z[ρ]/(integer scale), ρ = √(qn·qd), by the integer-scaled three-term recurrence (no gcd, no floating point), replaces ρ by an integer enclosure (isqrt, 40 digits) and floors to 10^−30; the tail k > 600 uses Lemma T: g_k ≥ (9/25)[5 − 2S/(K0+2)] with S = Σ_{i<j}(1−t_ij)^{−1} ≤ 12.2591, giving g_k ≥ 1.78533 for k > 600. Result (7 s):
  γ(F_B^exact) ≥ 1.776009937964733  (exact rational 177600993796473305296398794157/10^29; minimum over k at k = 9; the five near-tied degrees 9, 16, 12, 4, 8 all lie in [1.7760099380, 1.7760099493]).
Hence, unconditionally,
  **Γ_3 ≥ 1.776009937964733 > 77/45 = 1.7111 > 3/2**,
and by §4(a),(c) Γ_3 is attained by an N = 5 frame; the ONB and the simplex are both non-optimal in d = 3. (Test of the certificate pipeline: the triangular bipyramid gives exactly 1.545185185185… = cycle-1's 1.5452.)

### 5.4 What is not proved for d = 3
The upper bound is only the ceiling Γ_3 ≤ 9/5 (Corollary §2). Whether B is the global maximiser is NUMERICAL: see the multistart statistics below. An upper bound Γ_3 ≤ sup_F min(g_4,g_8,g_9,g_12,g_16) over the 4-dimensional family would need a certified global optimisation of a min of polynomials of degree ≤ 16 in the Gram entries (e.g. interval branch-and-bound on the (n_1,n_2,n_3,ψ)-box or an SOS/Lasserre relaxation); not attempted here.
