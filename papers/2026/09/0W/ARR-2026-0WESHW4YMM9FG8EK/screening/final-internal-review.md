## Analytic checks

**Central radical and group size.** For a label $au+bv$, commuting with u and v is exactly the pair of conditions $q\mid b$ and $q\mid a$. A relation $au+bv=0$ satisfies those same divisibility conditions. Thus the relation lattice is contained in $q\mathbb Z^2$, the radical is $qH=\langle qu,qv\rangle$, and $|H/qH|=q^2$. The argument remains valid for $q=1$, including collinear or dependent supports. It does not presume a prime modulus or freely generated labels.

**Equal multiplicity and cyclic copies.** Character averaging gives each central joint space dimension $d/h$. The two central powers generate the central label group after rephasing, so distinct characters have distinct ordered eigenvalue pairs. On one such space, V bijects the q eigenspaces of U. Choosing an orthonormal basis of one eigenspace and following its V orbits constructs $m=d/(qh)$ identical orthogonal q-dimensional copies. This proves both integrality and equal multiplicity; neither follows merely from the trace of U being zero. The $q=1$ branch is a scalar block of multiplicity m, as explicitly stated.

**Determinant, signs and singular nullity.** For $q>1$, a diagonal matrix plus one full cyclic shift has only the diagonal and full-cycle permutation terms in its determinant. Their sum is $c^q-(-1)^q(a^qs+b^qt)$. The kernel recurrence divides by the nonzero shift weights and b, so nullity in one copy is at most one even when a diagonal entry vanishes or $c=0$. Singularity gives exactly one kernel dimension. Raising the determinant to m and counting the repeated copies proves (3)–(4). The commuting central polynomial in (4a) is zero on an entire qm-dimensional sector, which explains the factor $1/q$; it is not an identification of the two kernels as subspaces. For $q=1$ the displayed bracket correctly becomes $c+as+bt$.

**Coefficient and degeneracy conditions.** The circle argument permits at most two pairs only when $abc\ne0$. The manuscript retains this condition in (4b) while correctly allowing $c=0$ in the exact rank formulas. A scalar central power leaves at most one possible pair. For exponent-two centers, two distinct sign-corner zeros force one of the three coefficients to vanish, giving the stated improvement to m. The cyclic order-four exception is correctly distinguished from the exponent-two order-four group.

**Arithmetic formula and prime powers.** The index of the lattice generated by u,v and the two d-coordinate vectors is the gcd of its maximal minors. Substitution gives $m=G/g$. Writing $d=gq$ and $\delta=g\delta_1$ with $\gcd(q,\delta_1)=1$ gives exactly the gcd in (4c). Since G and g depend only on labels, the formula is lift independent; the separate replay also checks changed negative and positive lifts. For $d=p^k$ and $q>1$, $\delta/g$ is a p-adic unit, so m equals one. This justifies every retained downstream use of the noncommuting prime-power nullity bound.

**Sharpness and negative control.** In dimension 36, the phase i in $V=iX^{-3}Z^{18}$ is essential: it makes $V^2=X^{-6}$, while $U^2=X^6$. The six central characters satisfy $t=s^{-1}$, and exactly the two primitive sixth roots obey $s+s^{-1}=1$. Thus q=2, h=6, m=3 and nullity six are correct. In dimension 12, $X+XZ^6=X(I+Z^6)$ has nullity six, while q=2, h=6, m=1. All six sectors are singular because $V^2=-U^2$. This genuinely refutes dropping the nonzero-constant hypothesis from the two-sector bound while satisfying the exact formula.

## Antecedents and limits

The new standard-machinery citation was checked directly against [Farenick, Ojo and Plosker, *Universality of Weyl Unitaries*, Lemmas 2.1–2.2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.00129). It does contain the repeated-eigenvalue block form for finite-order Weyl pairs. The manuscript gives its own V-orbit proof and does not attribute a new representation classification to itself. [Gheorghiu, *Standard Form of Qudit Stabilizer Groups*, Theorem 1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1101.1519), supplies the cited dimension/group-order relation and trace-projector proof in composite dimension. This check supports the stated antecedent map, not external priority of the rank applications.

The universal common multiplicity is confined to the fixed cyclic Weyl representation; arbitrary q-commuting unitaries can have unequal central multiplicities. The prime-power global rank lists do not become global composite rank lists merely from (4). The exact pure-probe, one-use list-decoding assumptions and the strict low-rank cutoff remain explicit. No proof-assistant formalization, exhaustive bibliographic-priority review, independent human refereeing or calibrated ARR score is claimed. Within those limits, the final delta and its identified PDF pass this separate internal review without requested mathematical corrections.
