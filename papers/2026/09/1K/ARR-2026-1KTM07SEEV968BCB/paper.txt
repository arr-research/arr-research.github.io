1.3 Calibration
Theorem A is classical [MCJ73], and the scalar envelope Theorem Z is the known Delvaux–Van Barel
theorem [DVB06, DVB08]; Section 4.2 supplies a self‑contained proof through valuation reduction and
digit weight. Lemma R is the classical minimum‑distance statement for the dual incidence code of the
afϐine plane of prime order, stated here over any characteristic‑𝑝ϐield with an elementary proof; see
[KMM08, Section 2, Result 1]. The contribution proposed here is the extension to arbitrary complex
combinations of cyclic Weyl operators through sparsity 2𝑝−1, the valuation formulation B′, and the
primitive‑direction/torsion‑coset mechanism N. The search for prior operator results is bounded and
does not certify worldwide priority. Theorems T2 and T3 are short consequences of trace orthogonality
and of the imported [0WE] rank rule, respectively; T3 remains conditional. Veriϐication coverage is
itemised in Section 7. The supplied history includes author‑only runs, separately implemented checks
and unrepeated computations, not a universal independent replay.
