## Evolution of the mathematical target

The first target was the stratum below 2d/3: twelve flat line states and fifty-four crossing segments. Direct examination of the factor square showed a stronger cutoff. Below 7d/9, every allowed factor still has a relative support group of order three, so its square is a positive combination of at most two parallel line projectors. This permits the additional parallel edges and triangle interiors. The cutoff is attained by a three-term order-nine polynomial outside the nine-cell algebra.

The 150-family classification is not inferred from finite matrix samples. Its universal proof uses a strict threshold comparison and the complete classification of small affine-line unions. The exact finite replay independently builds the original cyclic matrices, while the exhaustive line-subset enumeration checks all combinatorial families, including those excluded by the theorem.

The new square-length statement concerns the minimum number of factors, which differs from list size. For example, a triangle state needs three factors but still permits three-label lists; a parallel-edge state needs one factor but usually needs three-label lists. Only the twelve parallel midpoints permit two labels.

The rank-4d/9 construction is a separate four-term factor, `(I-U)(I-V)`, and is normalized by trace 4d. The three-list obstruction follows from the proven rank gap. This establishes a genuine exact-rank nonmonotonicity and is not a statement about a Schmidt-rank budget.

## Rejected extensions and limitations

- Applying the theorem at d=3 would discard the required commutation and nine equal character cells. A rank-two mixture of two noncommuting order-three eigenprojectors already shows a different geometry.
- Extending the same commuting-algebra conclusion to rank 7d/9 fails. The order-nine polynomial square has a nonzero Weyl coefficient outside the order-three subgroup.
- Extending the individual three-term rank list to positive sums fails at rank 5d/9. The crossing mixture is an explicit counterexample.
- Treating factor count as list size or as minimum POVM outcome count is invalid. The definitions and separate minima are stated explicitly.
- A formula for all higher-rank three-list states, approximate rigidity, other odd primes, and minimum list size at dimension-nine ranks one and two was not established here.
