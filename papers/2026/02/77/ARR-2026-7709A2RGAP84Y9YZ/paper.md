## Abstract

We establish that the orbit space B = A/G of SU(N_c) lattice gauge theory satisfies the Riemannian curvature-dimension condition RCD*(N_c/4, dim A); in particular, it satisfies CD(N_c/4, infinity) in the sense of Lott-Villani-Sturm. The proof shows that the configuration space A = SU(N_c)^{|B_1(Lambda)|}, with the bi-invariant product metric = -2 tr(XY), is an Einstein manifold with Ric_A = (N_c/4) g_A (Proposition 2.2), and applies the stability of the RCD* condition under quotients by compact groups of measure-preserving isometries (Galaz-Garcia-Kell-Mondino-Sosa). This bypasses O'Neill computations and handles the singular stratum (reducible connections) automatically. As a consequence we derive a conditional log-Sobolev inequality for measures d mu = e^{-Phi} d nu / Z with constant alpha = (N_c/4) e^{-osc(Phi)}. All constants are computed explicitly for SU(2) and SU(3). This provides the geometric input in a program aiming at a volume-uniform log-Sobolev inequality for SU(N_c) lattice Yang-Mills theory at weak coupling; the complementary analytic input is developed in the companion papers cited in Section 6.1.
