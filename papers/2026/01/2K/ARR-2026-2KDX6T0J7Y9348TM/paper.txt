Version 3 - supersession and correction note
Purpose of this replacement
This replacement preserves public version 2 and makes the series relation explicit. The exact-diagonalization
benchmark remains a valid finite-size component, but ai.viXra:2601.0051v2 is the consolidated source because it
retains that benchmark and adds tensor-network ladder calculations and their scope controls.
The observed Petz/Wilson rank alignment is not upgraded to a confinement-specific or thermodynamic theorem. Its
absolute values remain prescription dependent, and the spectral-gap covariate leaves confinement specificity
unresolved at the tested sizes.
Citation rule
Cite ai.viXra:2601.0051v2 for the combined exact-diagonalization and tensor-network benchmark. Cite this record only
when discussing the standalone small-open-lattice exact-diagonalization component or its history.
Status boundary
This supersession is consolidation rather than a wholesale refutation. The finite exact-diagonalization data and stated
limitations survive; the successor is authoritative for the combined benchmark and later comparison claims.
The exact claim map and provenance record continue on notice page 2. The historical public manuscript then follows unchanged.

 CORRECTION AND CLAIM MAP
Results retained from the historical paper
- The n=8 small-open-lattice exact-diagonalization sweep and reported rank alignment remain finite-size numerical
evidence.
- The prescription dependence of absolute Petz-type recovery errors remains an explicit limitation.
- The equal correlation with the spectral gap means the tested data do not isolate confinement physics.
Claims superseded or narrowed
- This paper is no longer the preferred standalone citation for the programme's Petz-versus-Wilson benchmark.
- Any reading of the finite-size correlation as a universal confinement discriminator is excluded.
- The benchmark scope is superseded by the combined ED-plus-ladder presentation in the successor.
What the successor adds
- Tensor-network ladder calculations alongside the retained exact-diagonalization benchmark.
- Explicit separation between the ED BFS collar and the MPS-contiguous buffer proxy.
- Additional replication, stability and confinement-versus-gap limitations.
Evidence classification
Both records provide finite numerical benchmarks. They do not establish a thermodynamic confinement criterion,
universal recovery law or continuum theorem.
Provenance
Notice pages 1-2 are new. The following five pages reproduce public version 2 with identical rendered content. Frozen
copies of public v2 and successor v2 are included in the package.
