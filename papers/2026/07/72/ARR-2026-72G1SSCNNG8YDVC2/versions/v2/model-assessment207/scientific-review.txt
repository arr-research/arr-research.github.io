Crucially, the cited upper bound is correctly shifted. Ruiz-Antolin and Segura, Theorem 2, bound I_nu/I_(nu-1) by x/[nu-1/2 + sqrt((nu-1/2)^2+x^2)] for nu >= 1/2. 3. Strict positivity and quantifiers.

It proves unit-step monotonicity, not continuous monotonicity in order; the manuscript does not claim the latter.
