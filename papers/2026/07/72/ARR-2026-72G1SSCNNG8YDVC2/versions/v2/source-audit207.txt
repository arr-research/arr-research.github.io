3. NEW ABSOLUTE AND UNIFORM CONVERGENCE LEMMA
The DLMF10.25.2 positive power series, inspected directly, specializes to
I_j(beta)=sum_(h>=0)(beta/2)^(j+2h)/[h!(j+h)!] for integer j>=0.
Since (j+h)!>=j!, replacing its reciprocal yields the displayed upper
bound exp(beta^2/4)(beta/2)^j/j!. It is deliberately coarse but valid for
every fixed beta>0. I_1(beta)>0 is a fixed finite denominator, so r_k has
a factorial tail in k.
For k>=2, A_k is a polynomially weighted sum of products of shifted squared
r tails, and B_k=k r_(k-1)^4 has the same summability property. Factorial
decay dominates every fixed polynomial moment; finite low-index terms
including k=1 are harmless. In particular sum A_k,sum kA_k,sum B_k and
sum kB_k are finite. Positivity of the arrays gives
|c_mn|<=A_mB_n+A_nB_m. Each trigonometric bracket is bounded by2(m+n)
independently of real t. Summing that majorant over m<n is bounded by
finite products of the four moment sums, even if enlarged to all pairs.
The Weierstrass majorant therefore proves absolute convergence uniformly
on the whole real t axis for each fixed beta. No beta-uniform claim is
made. This permits reindexing/parity rewriting, not a term-sign conclusion.
