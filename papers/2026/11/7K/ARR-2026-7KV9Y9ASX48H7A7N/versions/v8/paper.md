# Product Marginals and Density-Moment Thresholds for Ising Transfer Kernels

Lluis Eriksson. Working paper, v8. No admission to the AIRR accepted collection or human peer review is claimed.

Fix a positive Ising coupling. We study finite Boolean positive transfer kernels with global weights. A code construction has uniform weighting and Perron stationary marginals up to linear order and exponentially many normalized eigenvalues approaching one. Separate product-tilt constructions and hypercontractive bounds identify density-moment boundaries. At s=(1+rho)/(1-rho), the exact norm-budget boundary is 2^(1/s), attained with any prescribed density floor below one. Escaped critical moment below one suffices for a uniform gap; for (s-1)/s<K<1 the exact universal strict-tail threshold is 1+[s^s/(s-1)^(s-1)](1-K)K^(s-1), including finite-cap attainment and necessity without a density floor. We solve the associated scalar moment envelope. Joint constructions retain the critical cap and uniform weighting-law marginals through every fixed power L^alpha, alpha<1/2. Conversely, critical-power uniform integrability and diverging marginal order imply operator-norm convergence of S_r=P_sqrt(rho) M_r P_sqrt(rho) to P_rho. A bounded critical norm suffices when k_L^2/(L log(L+1)) tends to infinity. These give eigenvalue and normalized-gap convergence for the isospectral transfer congruence T_r; norm convergence of T_r itself is not asserted. The code, tilt and spike families are distinct.
