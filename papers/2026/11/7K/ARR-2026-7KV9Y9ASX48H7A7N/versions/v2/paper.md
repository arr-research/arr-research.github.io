# Product Marginals and Density-Moment Thresholds for Ising Transfer Kernels

Lluis Eriksson. Working paper, v2. No admission to the AIRR accepted collection or human peer review is claimed.

For fixed Boolean noise parameter rho, a density moment of order s>(1+rho)/(1-rho) gives an explicit dimension-independent normalized transfer gap, allowing zeros and requiring neither a pointwise cap nor a positive floor. Every strict subcritical order admits exponentially collapsing gaps for all fixed B>1 despite density at least1/B. The critical endpoint remains open. This complements the code construction with product marginals for both weighting and Perron stationary laws and exponentially many slow modes. The threshold and ingredients are classical; external priority is unestablished. No local Gibbs or physical Yang-Mills result follows.
