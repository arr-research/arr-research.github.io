# Product Marginals and Density-Moment Thresholds for Ising Transfer Kernels

Lluis Eriksson. Working paper, v7. No admission to the AIRR accepted collection or human peer review is claimed.

Fix a positive Ising coupling. We construct positive Boolean weights with uniform labelled marginals up to linear order while exponentially many normalized transfer eigenvalues approach one; the Perron stationary law has the same marginals. Separate product-tilt constructions and hypercontractive bounds identify the density-moment boundary for a dimension-independent normalized transfer gap. At s=(1+rho)/(1-rho), the exact norm-budget boundary is 2^(1/s). Escaped critical moment below one suffices for a uniform gap, while one collapsing family satisfies every tail cap one at cutoffs at least one and total critical moment at most two. Stable spikes show that critical-power uniform integrability is not necessary. For (s-1)/s<K<1 we determine the exact universal strict-tail threshold 1+C_s(1-K)K^(s-1), where C_s=s^s/(s-1)^(s-1). Every smaller budget gives an explicit dimension-independent gap for arbitrary nonnegative mean-one densities, without a density floor; an existing construction attains collapse at the threshold with floor K. Reverse hypercontractivity, normalized sign compression and truncation prove necessity. We also solve the scalar total-moment envelope. The code, tilt and spike families are distinct. External priority is unestablished. No fixed-range Gibbs, local-dynamics, physical-gap or continuum conclusion is claimed.
