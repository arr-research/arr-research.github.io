# Product Marginals and Density-Moment Thresholds for Ising Transfer Kernels

Lluis Eriksson. Working paper, v9. No admission to the AIRR accepted collection or human peer review is claimed.

We compare exact marginal agreement and density-moment control for finite Boolean Ising transfer operators with global weights. Code-based weights preserve uniform labelled marginals on a positive fraction of coordinates while exponentially many normalized transfer eigenvalues approach one. Separate constructions and hypercontractive estimates determine the critical norm-budget boundary and a sharp strict-tail threshold. At the critical exponent s=(1+rho)/(1-rho), positive densities under the exact norm cap 2^(1/s), with any prescribed density floor below one, can match every marginal through k_L=o(sqrt(L)) while their first two transfer eigenvalues tend to one. Conversely, any bounded critical norm and k_L/sqrt(L) tending to infinity imply operator-norm convergence of S_r=P_sqrt(rho) M_r P_sqrt(rho) to P_rho and spectral-gap convergence for the isospectral transfer congruence T_r. Norm convergence of T_r itself is not asserted. An entropy-deficit argument removes the logarithmic loss and identifies the exact vanishing scale for the endpoint high-degree noise norm. At fixed k_L/sqrt(L) tending to C>0, odd Fourier erasure and a one-sided positivity estimate yield an explicit attained gap profile with a quadratic small-C upper bound. At the same critical cap and with a fixed positive density floor 0<b<1, a mean-reserved tail-moment budget and three-degree-block decoupling prove a uniform positive normalized gap for every fixed C>0. For each such fixed floor, the lower and attained upper bounds give quadratic order of the asymptotic gap infimum as C decreases to zero, after the dimension limit; optimal constants remain open.
