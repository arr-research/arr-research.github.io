# Product Marginals and Density-Moment Thresholds for Ising Transfer Kernels

Lluis Eriksson. Working paper, v5. No admission to the AIRR accepted collection or human peer review is claimed.

Fix a positive Ising coupling. We construct positive Boolean weights with uniform labelled marginals up to linear order while exponentially many normalized transfer eigenvalues approach one; the Perron stationary law has the same marginals. Separate product-tilt constructions and hypercontractive bounds identify the density-moment boundary for a dimension-independent normalized transfer gap. At s=(1+rho)/(1-rho), the exact norm-budget boundary is 2^(1/s). We prove that escaped critical moment Theta<1 suffices for a uniform gap, with an explicit finite-cutoff bound. One collapsing family simultaneously satisfies every K-tail moment cap one for K>=1 and total critical moment cap two, with any prescribed positive density floor below one. For each fixed 0<=K<1, the same tail cap guarantees a positive gap. Stable one-vertex spikes realize every finite positive Theta, so critical-power uniform integrability is sufficient but not necessary. The code, tilt and spike families are distinct. Classical hypercontractivity, sign compression, product tilts and rank-one perturbations supply the mechanisms. Positive constants are not claimed optimal; external priority is unestablished. No fixed-range Gibbs, local-dynamics, physical-gap or continuum conclusion is claimed.
