# Product Marginals and Density-Moment Thresholds for Ising Transfer Kernels

Lluis Eriksson. Working paper, v4. No admission to the AIRR accepted collection or human peer review is claimed.

For fixed Boolean noise parameter rho, a density moment of order s>(1+rho)/(1-rho) gives an explicit dimension-independent normalized transfer gap, allowing zeros and requiring neither a pointwise cap nor a positive floor. Every strict subcritical order admits exponentially collapsing gaps for all fixed B>1 despite density at least1/B. At the critical exponent, Theorem13 identifies the exact norm-budget boundary B*=2^(1/s). Below it a positive dimension-independent gap exists; at equality and above, gaps can collapse even with any prescribed fixed floor below one. The sufficient positive-gap formula is not claimed optimal, and no equality convergence rate is asserted. The earlier B>2 rate remains a separate quantitative result. This complements the code construction with product marginals for both weighting and Perron stationary laws and exponentially many slow modes. The threshold and ingredients are classical; external priority is unestablished. No local Gibbs or physical Yang-Mills result follows.
