F1 — explicit correction to the preliminary heat-bath comparison on page5

The sentence immediately before Proposition5 must be read with the following qualification:
For L>=2 and the continuous-time single-site heat-bath generator
  G f = sum_i c_i ( E_mu[f | all coordinates except i] - f ),
with finite positive site rates c_i fixed as t varies, the gap tends to zero as |t| tends to1 at fixed L.

The omitted L>=2 qualifier matters. For L=1 the update redraws the entire one-spin law and the continuous-time gap is c_1, independent of t. We accept this correction rather than asserting that the unqualified sentence holds at L=1.

For L>=2, put f_i(x)=(-1)^(x_i). Under the positive parity law mu_t, E f_i=0 and Var(f_i)=1. Its conditional mean given other coordinates is t times their parity, so E Var(f_i | other coordinates)=1-t^2. All heat-bath updates except i leave f_i unchanged. Its Dirichlet quotient is therefore c_i(1-t^2), giving gap(-G)<=c_i(1-t^2). This proves the stated limit under the fixed-rate convention. For fixed random-scan probabilities the corresponding discrete-time quotient has the same factor.

Proposition5 already explicitly assumes L>=2. Its transfer spectrum and tanh(2beta) ratio ceiling are unchanged, as are Theorem1, its exponentially many slow transfer modes, the stationary marginal matching and Corollary6. This correction concerns a preliminary comparison sentence, not a premise of those proofs. The first reviewer identified no unresolved material objection to those main claims.
