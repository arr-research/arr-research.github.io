1 Introduction
NoGois the two-player combinatorial placement
game derived fromGoby forbidding both captures
(including self-captures) and passes [3, 6]. Fol-
lowing the PSPACE-completeness of planarGraph
NoGo[3] andDots & Boxes[1], Burke, Davies,
and Tennenhouse [2] settled two major open ques-
tions:
1.Square-grid normal & mis` ere play (Theorem 1.1):
Realizing B2CL gadgets (Goal,Variable,Or,
And/Split,Choice) directly onn×ngrids
with insulated White wires, frozen liberties, and
a checkerboard White reserve yields PSPACE-
completeness on positions reachable from the
empty grid under both normal and mis` ere play.
2.Subcubic bipartite graphs (Theorem 1.2 &
Lemma 4.1):IntroducingCoins-are-Lava(a
Strings-and-Coins [5] variant forbidding isolating
any coin), proving its PSPACE-completeness
from Schaefer’s positive-CNF game via odd rope
widthsN= 2t+ 3, and reducing to connected
bipartite graphs of maximum degree ∆≤3.
