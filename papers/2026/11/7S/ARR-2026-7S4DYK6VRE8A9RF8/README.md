# Weight sensitivity of leadership in the CAIDP AI Index

Lluis Eriksson. Working paper v1.

Canada and Japan share the highest numerical score in both the 2025 and 2026 CAIDP AI Index. We ask how much the twelve item weights must differ before another country can tie them. The exact largest-to-smallest weight ratio falls from 3 to 1.5. The same result holds among the 80 countries present in both editions. Replacing only the leaders' response to the UNESCO implementation item reproduces the fall under either vintage of the other countries' responses. Replacing the other countries' responses alone changes the set of potential entrants but leaves the critical ratio unchanged. The comparison describes the geometry of the published evaluations.
