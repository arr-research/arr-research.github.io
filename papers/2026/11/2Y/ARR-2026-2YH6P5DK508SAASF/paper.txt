1 Introduction and Scope
Following Harvey and van der Hoeven’s O(nlogn ) integer
multiplication algorithm [4] and their reduction showing
multiplication is at least as hard as matrix transposition [5],
determining the optimal logarithmic exponent on deter-
ministic multitape Turing machines has remained central
to computational complexity. In its November 24, 2026
unrefereed preprint [8], OpenAI establishes that this ex-
ponent is 1/2: a single fixed multitape machine multiplies
n-bit integers (and computes exact quotients and integer
square roots, Cor. 6.1) in n(1 + logn )1/2+o(1) steps and
O(n) visited cells (Theorems 1.1 and 5.1), while under
variable-count Weak ETH any fixed deterministic multi-
tape multiplier, divider, or integer square-root machine
requires Ω(m√logm ) time (Theorem 1.2, Cor. 6.2, Theo-
rem 8.1).
The upper bound combines odd-characteristic spectral
swap diagonalization (Sec. 2.4), Lemma 3.1’s guarded eight-
shift bulk XOR, Cor. 3.2’s two-involution position permu-
tations, Lemma 3.3’s (2 d− 1)-stage Beneˇ s [1] block rout-
ing, Eq. (5.3)’s triangular scaled-residue CRT layout, and
Lemma 5.2’s Bluestein [ 2] chirp conjugation on borrowed
coordinates without exponential padding. The conditional
lower bound rules out characteristic-zero ports via sun-
flower 3-SAT sparsification [6] (Theorem 7.1) and prices
N×N partial transposition in CAT(0) spectral spaces [9, 7]
via barycentric variance and entropy-wire extraction (Lem-
mas 8.2–8.3, Theorem 8.1), then transfers the bound to
division and floor square roots via exact Brent-style reduc-
tions [3] (Cor. 6.2).
Honest Scope Boundary.This companion artifact
machine-checks finite algebraic identities, exact bijections,
matrix conjugations, rational arithmetic inequalities, and
finite CNF/metric witnesses extracted from [ 8]. It does
notformalize multitape head trajectories, Levin universal
search scheduling (Sec. 6.3), Baker–Harman–Pintz prime
gaps (Sec. 5.1), or asymptotic CAT(0) Poincar´ e obstruc-
tions (Secs. 7–8).
