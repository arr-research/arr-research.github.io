\begin{abstract}
This archival technical note provides a self-contained, auditable claim-to-theorem
specification and continuation handoff for the Yang--Mills Lean~4 formalization inside
\texttt{THE-ERIKSSON-PROGRAMME} at archival release \texttt{yang-mills-archive-v1.0}
(commit \texttt{6b54eca695e96b3038ceda18276c4e28fb45983f}; canonical Lean core checkpoint
\texttt{02fae0ad} in \texttt{project-state.json}, Lean \texttt{v4.29.0-rc6}, pinned
\texttt{mathlib} \texttt{07642720480157414db592fa85b626dafb71355b}). Responding to every
material objection raised against v1 (\texttt{ARR-2026-7DYZAEKGJP8FPTJ1} v1), this v2
revision replaces directory-level summaries with exact fully-qualified Lean~4 theorem
statements, explicit coupling and geometric hypotheses, concrete strictly-interacting
non-vacuity witnesses, exact operator classes and volume quantifiers for the certified
obstructions, a strict distinction between \texttt{YangMillsCore}-compiled,
\texttt{oracle\_check}-audited, and standalone declarations, and resolved AIRR identifiers
for all 32 indexed mathematical-physics records.

We state the mathematical boundary without pseudo-metrics. The verified core proves
fixed-spacing lattice results: a Koteck\'y--Preiss / Mayer--Ursell polymer engine;
volume-uniform strong-coupling $\mathrm{SU}(N_c)$ Wilson-loop area laws (indexed by
$\mathbb{Z}/N_c\mathbb{Z}$-chain homology area \texttt{chainAreaA}) and exponential
clustering of truncated two-plaquette correlations without Peter--Weyl; an explicit
strictly-interacting $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ non-vacuity window $0 < |\beta| \le 10^{-5}$; exact
$\mathbb{Z}_n$ centre-symmetry selection rules for positively-oriented Wilson loops with
$n \nmid |es|$; conditional 4D marginal-coupling UV-summation theorems requiring
$\kappa_0 > 1$; real-order Amos/Bessel ratio bounds on $\nu \ge 0$; and a transfer-matrix
identification chain for the 1D open $\Z_2$ Ising spin chain. No four-dimensional
continuum limit ($a \to 0$), no physical $\mathrm{SU}(N)$ Osterwalder--Schrader or
Wightman reconstruction, and no solution to the Clay Millennium Yang--Mills Mass Gap
problem is claimed. We separate open formalization tasks (fixed-spacing $\mathrm{SU}(N)$
Wilson reflection positivity and the Balaban/Dimock \texttt{hRpoly} estimate in draft
PR~\#29) from open mathematics (4D continuum Yang--Mills existence and mass gap).
\end{abstract}

\section{Verification Architecture and Coverage Boundary}

To eliminate ambiguity between built modules, audited declarations, and standalone work,
the archival release \texttt{yang-mills-archive-v1.0} maintains three distinct automated
boundaries:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Core compilation and axiom oracle (\texttt{lean-build.yml}).}
  On every push and PR to \texttt{main} (with a cache-free \texttt{cold\_build: true}
  manual dispatch option), GitHub Actions installs Lean \texttt{v4.29.0-rc6}, fetches
  prebuilt \texttt{mathlib} \texttt{.olean} caches for commit
  \texttt{07642720480157414db592fa85b626dafb71355b}, runs \texttt{lake build YangMillsCore}
  (recorded core count: $8466$ jobs in \texttt{project-state.json}), builds the modules
  imported by \texttt{oracle\_check.lean}, executes \texttt{lake env lean oracle\_check.lean},
  and verifies via \texttt{scripts/check\_oracle\_output.py} that every \texttt{\#print axioms}
  readout depends only on \texttt{[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]}.
  \item \textbf{Lexical honesty guard (\texttt{ci.yml}).}
  \texttt{scripts/check\_consistency.py} scans \texttt{YangMills/} and \texttt{Lean/}
  (excluding \texttt{YangMills/Experimental/}) for \texttt{sorry}, and scans the verified-core
  directories (\texttt{L0\_Lattice}, \texttt{L1\_GibbsMeasure}, \texttt{KP},
  \texttt{ClayCore}, \texttt{Paper}, \texttt{RG}, \texttt{YangMillsCore.lean}, and core
  \texttt{P8\_PhysicalGap} Haar/compactness files) for \texttt{axiom}. A lexical scan alone
  never certifies compilation or non-vacuity; it is strictly supplementary to
  \texttt{lean-build.yml} and the non-vacuity witnesses below.
  \item \textbf{Standalone and branch-only modules (explicitly outside \texttt{YangMillsCore}).}
  The finite $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ Wilson cylinder geometry/measure modules (R1/R2, Sept 2026)
  and draft PR~\#29 (\texttt{hRpoly}) are \emph{not} imported by \texttt{YangMillsCore.lean}
  and are \emph{not} covered by the core build guarantee.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{KP / Mayer polymer engine (\texttt{YangMills/KP/})}

\subsection{Strong-coupling $\mathrm{SU}(N_c)$ area law, IR clustering, and non-vacuity (\texttt{YangMills/L1\_GibbsMeasure/})}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Volume-uniform exact-action Wilson-loop area law:}
  \texttt{YangMills.normalized\_exp\_wilson\_loop\_area\_law}
  (\texttt{L1\_GibbsMeasure/RestrictedGate.lean:2045}). For any dimension $d$, lattice side
  $N$, gauge rank $N_c \ge 1$ (\texttt{[NeZero N\_c]}), arbitrary edge list
  $es : \mathrm{List}\;(\mathrm{ConcreteEdge}\;d\;N)$, complex plaquette couplings
  $\|c_p\| \le \delta$ with $c_p' = \overline{c_p}$, and parameters $t,\varepsilon \ge 0$,
  $\sigma \in [0,1]$ satisfying the explicit volume-independent KP smallness inequalities
  \[
    (16d+1)^2 (e^{2\delta N_c} - 1) e^{t+\varepsilon+1} < 1, \quad
    (16d)\frac{(e^{2\delta N_c}-1)e^{t+\varepsilon+1}}{1 - (16d+1)^2(e^{2\delta N_c}-1)e^{t+\varepsilon+1}} \le t,
  \]
  together with $(16d+1)^2 \sigma < 1$ and the effective-activity bound
  $(e^{2\delta N_c}-1)\exp(\cdots) \le \sigma^2$, the normalized expectation of
  $\mathrm{tr}(U_{es})$ under the exact exponential plaquette action
  $\prod_p \exp(c_p\,\mathrm{tr}\,U_p + \overline{c_p}\,\overline{\mathrm{tr}\,U_p})$
  obeys
  \[
    \bigl|\langle \mathrm{tr}\, U_{es}\rangle\bigr|
    \;\le\; N_c\, \exp\bigl(4d\,|\mathrm{edgeSupport}(es)|\,C_{\mathrm{perim}}\bigr)\;
    \sigma^{\,\mathrm{chainAreaA}_{\mathbb{Z}/N_c\mathbb{Z}}(\mathrm{loopChain}(es))},
  \]
  with constants independent of lattice side $N$. Because the exponent is the minimal
  spanning-surface area in $\mathbb{Z}/N_c\mathbb{Z}$-chain homology (\texttt{chainAreaA}),
  twice-traversed $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ loops have $\mathrm{loopChain}_{\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}} = 0$
  and hence $\mathrm{chainAreaA} = 0$ ($\sigma^0 = 1$), cleanly accommodating the exact
  Haar identity $\mathbb{E}[\mathrm{tr}(U_C^2)/2] = -1/2$ at $\beta = 0$.
  \item \textbf{Unconditional $\mathrm{SU}(N_c)$ two-plaquette exponential clustering without Peter--Weyl:}
  \texttt{YangMills.sun\_two\_plaquette\_correlator\_bound}
  (\texttt{L1\_GibbsMeasure/TwoPlaquetteCorrelator.lean:710}). For any $N_c \ge 1$, any
  measurable $f : \mathrm{SU}(N_c) \to \mathbb{R}$ with $|f| \le 1$, any $s > 0$,
  $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$, $t,\varepsilon \ge 0$ satisfying
  $(16d+1)^2 \bigl((e^{|\beta| N_c}-1) + s + (e^{|\beta| N_c}-1)s\bigr) e^{t+\varepsilon+1} < 1$
  and its paired KP smallness bound, and any two distinct plaquettes $p \neq q$ at
  touching-graph distance $2k \le \mathrm{dist}_{\mathrm{touchGraph}}(p,q)$, the connected
  two-plaquette correlator under the Wilson Gibbs measure with action
  $-\beta\,\mathrm{wilsonAction}(\mathrm{Re}\,\mathrm{tr})$ decays as
  $|\mathrm{Cov}_\beta(f(U_p), f(U_q))| \le C(d,N_c,\beta,s,t,\varepsilon)\,e^{-\varepsilon k}$,
  uniformly in lattice side $N$.
  \item \textbf{Explicit strictly-interacting non-vacuity witness:}
  \nolinkurl{YangMills/L1_GibbsMeasure/ThermodynamicNonvacuity.lean} proves that the
  uniform local KP regime is non-empty for the strictly interacting $\mathrm{SU}(2)$
  Wilson theory with nonconstant plaquette energy $\mathrm{Re}\,\mathrm{tr}\,U$ on the
  explicit interval $0 < |\beta| \le 10^{-5}$ with $t = \varepsilon = \eta = 10^{-2}$
  (and explicitly rules out degeneration to the free $\beta = 0$ point).
  \item \textbf{Fixed-spacing thermodynamic limit:}
  \nolinkurl{YangMills/L1_GibbsMeasure/IntegerThermodynamicLimit.lean} constructs a
  translation-invariant infinite-volume state on finite-support integer-lattice edge
  observables (\texttt{IntegerLocalObservable}) along periodic torus exhaustions of side
  $n+1 \to \infty$ at fixed spacing inside \texttt{UniformLocalKPRegime}, passing
  two-plaquette clustering to the limit (\nolinkurl{ThermodynamicCorrelation.lean}). This is
  an infinite-volume lattice limit, not an ultraviolet continuum limit.
  \item \textbf{Exact $\mathbb{Z}_n$ centre-symmetry selection rule:}
  \nolinkurl{YangMills.integral_wilsonLoopSU_gibbs_eq_zero}
  (\nolinkurl{L1_GibbsMeasure/GibbsSelectionRule.lean:120}). Under the global centre action
  multiplying every positively-oriented edge by $\omega = e^{2\pi i/n} I_n \in Z(\mathrm{SU}(n))$,
  every plaquette traverses two forward and two backward edges so the Wilson action is
  exactly invariant for all $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$. Any positively-oriented edge list $es$
  (\texttt{hpos}) transforms with eigenvalue $\omega^{|es|}$, so its interacting Gibbs
  expectation vanishes whenever $n \nmid |es|$. (Contractible plaquettes have signed net
  charge $0 \pmod n$ and are not forced to vanish.)
\end{itemize}

\section{Certified Negative Results and Exact Obstruction Classes}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Volume-uniform Poincar\'e walls (\texttt{ARR-2026-3Q0VH7PMNV8C9VQE}):}
  for the flat gauge-fixed Hodge plus block-averaging quadratic form
  $\langle A, K_0 A\rangle + a\|QA\|^2$ on the fine $d$-dimensional torus of side $L N'$
  ($d \ge 3$, $N_c \ge 2$, concrete $\mathfrak{su}(N_c)$ adjoint model), every flat
  block-Poincar\'e constant satisfies $C_P \ge L^{d-2}$, refuting any volume-uniform
  coercivity constant via this route. In the fluctuation sector on fine side $(2M)N'$,
  transported half-period square-wave modes satisfy $\|QA\|^2 \le (2M)^{-1}\|A\|^2$ and
  $\langle A, K_0 A\rangle = 8((2M)N')^{-1}\|A\|^2$, forcing $C_P \ge 2M/9$ for every
  $N' \ge 1$, $d \ge 3$, $N_c \ge 2$, and every adjoint model.
  \item \textbf{Flat lattice Hodge factorized-penalty and coarse-rank walls}
  (\texttt{ARR-2026-627PF1B4KK9VTRYT}, \texttt{ARR-2026-7EPM3V3DP796BBRR}, and expanded
  v2 dossier \texttt{papers/flat-coarse-rank/}): on flat periodic tori, divergence-zero
  exact-kernel Fourier families annihilated by the specified block-averaging map $Q$
  obstruct every penalty of the form $Q^* M Q$ factored through $Q$.
  \item \textbf{Spatial coupling and Birkhoff obstructions in $\Z_2$ slices}
  (\texttt{ARR-2026-4X084GVAYF87PB3F}, \texttt{ARR-2026-6M8PMVHTMH8YZ9ND},
  \texttt{ARR-2026-1A608PTGTC9XX9JZ}): isolates where uniform-vacuum and extent-independent
  bounds fail under spatial coupling.
  \item \textbf{Parity barriers for decoupling inequalities (\texttt{ARR-2026-5HRC3PS9HZ9WDAA5}):}
  no comparison functional of bounded marginal order can certify uniform decoupling.
  \item \textbf{Sine-series Wronskian counterexamples (\texttt{ARR-2026-5FKM2GGK348CPTE9} v2):}
  explicit finite arrays $a=(3,4,3), b=(3,2,1)$ ($W(2\pi/3)=3\sqrt{3}>0$) and
  $a=(6,10,24), b=(6,5,2)$ ($W(\pi-\varepsilon) = -8\varepsilon^3+O(\varepsilon^5)<0$ yet
  $W(2\pi/3)=51\sqrt{3}>0$) refute unrestricted sine-series Biernacki--Krzy\.z and generic
  local-to-global lifting.
  \item \textbf{Two-transporter Haar projection no-go (\texttt{docs/SU2-TWO-TRANSPORTER-NOGO-20260731.md}):}
  two free Haar-integrated transporters absent from the observable project the reduced
  $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ quadratic form to the trivial sector.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Open Formalization Tasks vs.\ Open Mathematics}

We separate bounded formalization tasks (classically known mathematics not yet formalized
in this Lean archive) from genuinely open mathematics:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Open formalization task — physical $\mathrm{SU}(N)$ Wilson reflection
  positivity at fixed spacing:} classically proved by Osterwalder and Seiler (1978), but
  still open in this Lean archive beyond the standalone R1/R2 finite $\mathrm{SU}(2)$
  cylinder geometry and normalized product-Haar measure
  (\texttt{docs/evidence/physical-cylinder-r2-20260906/}, outside \texttt{YangMillsCore}).
  \item \textbf{Open formalization / constructive-QFT task — \texttt{hRpoly} (frozen in
  draft PR~\#29):} concrete Balaban/Dimock cluster-expansion-with-holes remainder activity
  decay for the physical gauge RG operator. Frozen checkpoint (\texttt{docs/HRPOLY-STATUS.md}):
  $20/41$ terminal \texttt{PreEq136} producers, $\mathrm{TermSource}=0$, uniform physical
  $(B_0,\delta_0)$ and regional transport (F5) open.
  \item \textbf{Open mathematics — 4D continuum limit ($a \to 0$) and OS/Wightman
  reconstruction with positive mass gap for $\mathrm{SU}(N)$ ($N \ge 2$):} no complete
  proof exists in the literature or in this repository; removing the UV regulator while
  retaining a non-perturbative mass gap remains the Clay Millennium Problem.
\end{enumerate}
