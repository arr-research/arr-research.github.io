Stationary marginals and bottleneck restrictions for Boolean Doob
                            kernels
                                              Lluis Eriksson

                                            14 November 2026


                                                 Abstract
         We study positive multiplicative transforms of the Boolean noise kernel whose actual station-
     ary density has exact low-order marginals. A finite-exponent moment bound gives quantitative
     convergence of the smoothed potential, the Perron normalization, and the weighting density in
     Hellinger distance. This conclusion cannot be strengthened to uniform convergence of the po-
     tential: a product of block majorities yields an admissible counterexample even with a bounded
     stationary density. At the critical moment exponent and square-root matching scale, a compar-
     ison of cut energies shows that every sufficiently low-conductance cut has exponentially small
     stationary mass. The comparison uses a previously proved weighting-density theorem only on
     an auxiliary operator; it does not identify that operator with the actual Doob kernel. A sepa-
     rate bound excludes almost-unit killed modes on exponentially small-cardinality supports in an
     explicit noise range, without requiring marginal matching. The dimension-uniform stationary
     spectral-gap question remains open. We give full proofs of these linked results and state the
     external and programme dependencies explicitly.

7    Classical scaling and the unresolved comparison
The relation hP h = π is a symmetric finite Schrödinger scaling. General finite scaling existence
is classical, and Lemma 2 supplies an elementary specialization rather than a new existence prin-
ciple. Georgiou–Pavon [3] obtain a finite-dimensional contraction through a projective diameter.
Duan–Lyu–Powell [4, Theorems2.1–2.5] distinguish coupling stability from potential stability; their
discrete supremum estimate requires entrywise relative margin closeness and a conditioning pa-
rameter. Our hypothesis is exact low-order matching, not entrywise closeness of the full margin.
These results are contextual comparisons, not premises for a dimension-uniform conclusion here.
The cited passages were checked; no complete priority survey is claimed.
    Theorem 1 narrows the obstruction to the original gap question: Perron normalization and a
persistent total-variation mismatch are controlled. Theorem 3 prevents resolving the remaining
problem by assuming uniform potential convergence. Theorem 5 gives direct actual-kernel control
above a shrinking mass scale; Theorem 6 excludes certain small supports directly. These restrictions
                                                                                              √
do not exhaust the remaining sets: from the floor, |A| ≤ 2L ν(A)/b, which at ν(A) of order e−c L still
permits cardinality far above eρL/1920 . What remains is relative energy control for the uncovered
stationary sets, or a fully admissible collapsing family. In particular, no bound on Ers , no exact
matching for r, and no full operator perturbation estimate has been derived. The supplement
records the failed routes so that these gaps are not hidden inside new hypotheses.
