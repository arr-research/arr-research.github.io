Disambiguations of Gap Hamming Distance

Abstract
Dahiya, Göös, Harms, Hatami, Hatami, Lovett, and
Yuan (arXiv:2611.32837, Nov. 2026) proved that ev-
ery total disambiguation of γ-Gap Hamming Distance
(γ-GHDn) requires randomized communication complex-
ityΩ( n2/3), establishing anO(1)versusΩ( n2/3)separa-
tion between randomized and pseudo-deterministic com-
munication complexity and improving Gavinsky’sΩ(√n)
parity decision tree bound toΩ(n2/3)—via an iterative
Marton-coupling and leaf-rectangle restoration argument
initially discovered by an AI model. Because such AI-
originated multi-round entropy-deficit arguments hinge on
delicate numerical constants (69/400 <1/5,27 /100 >1/4,
+7,+3, and2∆ T + mT ≤γn ), independent formal
verification is essential. We provide a zero-dependency
Lean 4.35.0formalization of 18 core structural, cou-
pling, leaf-restoration, and exponent-balance theorems
(zero sorry, zero warnings, zero Classical.choice)
paired with a zero-floating-point exact- Q certificate
verifier (GHD-DISAMBIG-CERT-1.0) across four modes:
Lemma 3.1 Marton sequential coupling and col(Z) ≤
∥µ∥∞, Lemma 4.2/Remark 4.3 leaf-rectangle restoration,
γ-GHD XOR-lift planted contradictions, and Theorem 1.1
multi-round recurrence trajectories. Across 16 canonical
certificates (0.104–2.537 ms), all invariants replay exactly
over Q while rejecting 63/63 live adversarial forgery at-
tacks.
1 Introduction
A central question in communication complexity asks how
much overhead is incurred by forcing a randomized proto-
col for a promise problem to bepseudo-deterministic[3],
i.e., to output a canonical answer with high probability
on every input—equivalently, to compute a total Boolean
disambiguationF:{0,1} n ×{0,1}n →{0,1}. Forγ-Gap
Hamming Distance (γ-GHDn, with constantγ∈ (0,1/2)),
the promise problem hasO(1)randomized communication
complexity via public-coin sampling, whereas Dahiya et
al. [1] proved that every total disambiguation requires

Notably, the authors report that the core proof strategy
of [1] was initially discovered by an AI model. Their
argument alternates two delicate rounds:
1. Marton Sequential Coupling and Coordinate
Planting (Lemma 3.1 & Lemma 4.1):Given a
product distributionµt ×νt on remaining coordinates
with min-entropy deficit≤D t and E[F] ≥1/2, Mar-
ton’s uniform coupling [6] yieldsE[dist(X,U)]2 ≤mD
and collision bound col(Z) ≤ ∥µ∥∞ (Eq. 5). Fix-
ing a ρ-fraction of coordinates drops remaining vari-
ables by(1 −ρ/2)while controlling union-bound error
2(1/20 + 1/32) + 1/100 = 69/400 <1/5, retaining ex-
pectation >1/4, and increasing min-entropy deficit by
at most+7.
2. Leaf Rectangle Restoration (Lemma 4.2 & Re-
mark 4.3):A d-bit protocol with error≤1/100parti-
tions the matrix into≤2d monochromatic rectangles;
flipping any1-leaf with conditional expectation<1/2
strictly lowers error and guarantees a1-leafA∗×B∗
of mass≥2−d/5with conditional expectation ≥1/2,
restoringE[F] ≥1/2at marginal blowup≤5·2d <2d+3
(total round deficit incrementd+ 10).
After T = O(ρ−1)rounds until mT ≤γn/ 2, every pair
(x,y)in the final support satisfiesdist(x,y) ≤2∆T+mT ≤
γn, forcingF≡ 0and contradicting E[F] ≥1/2whenever
d=o(n 2/3).

5 Conclusion
We delivered a self-contained, zero-floating-point verifica-
tion framework for theΩ(n2/3)Gap Hamming Distance
disambiguation lower bound of Dahiya et al. [1], combin-
ing 18 zero-sorry, zero-Classical.choice Lean 4.35.0
theorems with the exact-QGHD-DISAMBIG-CERT-1.0 ver-
ifier, 16 sub-3ms canonical certificates, and 63/63 live
adversarial forgery rejections. This demonstrates how
AI-discovered complexity proofs can be systematically de-
composed into auditable, machine-checked mathematical
artifacts.
