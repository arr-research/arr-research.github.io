Abstract
Miku Ishizaki and Jaeha Lee (arXiv:2611.33027, Nov.
2026) developed a locally deterministic matrix-polynomial
formulation of non-Markovian open quantum master equa-
tions P(D)E(t) = 0derived from the Laplace identity
P(s)(LE)(s) = Q(s), demonstrating exact order-5 differ-
ential equations and critical-parameter order reductions
for a coupled two-qubit model, a sharp three-tier sin-
gularity/periodicity hierarchy governed by odd/odd ra-
tional singular-value ratiosΩ−/Ω+, and order-3 polyno-
mial equations exhibiting a non-diagonalizable Jordan
exceptional point at λ = 2 γ0 in the unbounded multi-
mode Jaynes–Cummings model. We construct a stan-
dalone, zero-dependencyLean 4.35.0formalization of 18
core spectral, singularity, parity-obstruction, factorization,
and exceptional-point theorems (zerosorry, zero warn-
ings, zeroClassical.choice) paired with a zero-floating-
point exact-Q certificate verifier (POLY-MASTER-CERT-1.0)
across four modes. Across 16 canonical certificates (0.046–
0.648 ms, total3 .42 ms), all matrix-polynomial factoriza-
tions, Frobenius companion actions, irrational boundary
diagnostics, and Jordan nilpotency conditions replay ex-
actly overQ while rejecting 60/60 live adversarial forgery
attacks.
