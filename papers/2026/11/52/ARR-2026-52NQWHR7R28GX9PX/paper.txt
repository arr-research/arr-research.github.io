Explicit cocycles for graph Hall algebras
Lluis Eriksson
4 November 2026
Research draft, candidate 2: external assessment pending
Abstract
For the reduced simplicial set whose simplices are subgraphs of a finite labelled undi-
rected multigraph equipped with ordered partitionsof their vertices, we construct explicit
mod-two cohomology classes indexed by subgraphs. Vertex restriction pulls a supported
top-dimensional cochain into a global cocycle. Permutation cycles pair with these classes
as a Kronecker basis, while a vertex filtration bounds the total cohomology dimension
using augmented Boolean order complexes. Alexander–Whitney multiplication recov-
ers the graph Hall product. This proves the finite-graph Hall/cohomology identification
stated as Conjecture 1.1 by Toury. The construction concerns labelled subgraphs, not
isomorphism classes of rooted forests. Exact finite checks accompany the proof; priority
and external mathematical assessment remain pending.
1 The graph model and the statement
LetG= (V,E)be a finite labelled undirected graph. The finite edge set has an endpoint
map to unordered vertex pairs, allowing repeated endpoints (loops) and distinct labelled
edges with equal endpoint pairs. A subgraphHspecifiesS⊆Vand any subset of ambient
edge identities with both endpoints inS. It need not be induced inG. WriteH|A for the
induced restriction ofHtoA⊆V, and include the empty graph.

This statement addresses the finite-graph statement of [4, Conjecture 1.1]. It identifies
a cohomology algebra, not a homotopy type or the even-degree polyhedral-product space
constructed in that paper. No assertion is made about arbitrary reduced 2-Segal sets. In
particular, the construction does not pass to isomorphism classes of unlabelled graphs or to
a rooted-tree cut algebra.
2 A finite dimension bound
All chains and cochains below are normalized and have coefficients inF2. LetF pC∗(XG)
be generated by simplices whose underlying graph has at mostpvertices. This is a finite
filtration by subcomplexes: interior faces preserve the graph and endpoint faces decrease its
vertex set.
Lemma 2.The sum of the Betti numbers ofXG is at most the number of subgraphs ofG.
Proof.Forp>0, the quotientF pC∗/Fp−1C∗is a direct sum over subgraphsHwith exactly
pvertices. Each summand has the ordered partitions ofV(H)intoknonempty blocks as its
degree-kbasis. Its differential merges adjacent blocks; endpoint faces vanish in the quotient.
Whenp≥2, associate(B 1,...,B k)to the chain of proper nonempty subsets
B1 ⊊B1 ∪B2 ⊊···⊊B 1 ∪···∪B k−1.
This identifies the summand with the augmented simplicial chains of the proper Boolean
order complex, shifted up by two degrees. Fork= 1the chain of subsets is empty and
represents the augmentation in degree−1. Removing a proper initial union merges the
corresponding adjacent blocks, so the differentials agree overF2.
The proper Boolean order complex is the barycentric subdivision of the boundary of the
(p−1)-simplex. Its reduced homology is one-dimensional in degreep−2and zero otherwise.
The shifted summand therefore has one homology generator, in degreep. Forp= 1this
conclusion follows directly from the single degree-one partition. Forp= 0, the empty graph
contributes the basepoint in degree zero.
For a short exact sequence of finite chain complexes, the long exact sequence in homology
bounds the total homology dimension of the middle complex by the sum of those of the
other two. Apply this successively to the vertex filtration. Each subgraph contributes one
to the resulting upper bound. Normalized simplicial homology computes the homology of
the realization; over a field its Betti numbers equal those of cohomology.

The proof applies to finite labelled undirected multigraphs with loops overF2. For a
fixed subgraphH, the filtration summand depends only on its vertex set. Restriction retains
precisely the labelled edge identities whose endpoints survive. Thus the cycle cancellations,

cocycles and order exchange do not require simplicity. In the product argument every re-
