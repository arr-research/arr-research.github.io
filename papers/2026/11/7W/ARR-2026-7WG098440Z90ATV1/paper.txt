1 Introduction and Scope
Acap setin Fd
3 is a subset containing no three distinct
collinear points ( a + b + c = 0). By supermultiplicativ-
ity, the cap-set growth constant Λ = limd→∞ r3(d)1/d
exists, with upper bound Λ ≤ 2.7551 . . .[3] and succes-
sive constructive lower bounds by Edel (2 .217389) [ 2],
Tyrrell (2.2180) [ 7], and FunSearch [ 6]. In his Novem-
ber 23, 2026 unrefereed preprint [ 4], ZeHua Lu establishes
Λ > 2.229206468135675727981404 (Theorem 1.1) via a
1000-dimension single-slot closed-walk recursion on two-
color slots in F7
3, synchronized by port states (Lemma 3.1),
score intervals (Eqs. 3.1–3.2), and companion-path ex-
clusion (Prop. 3.2), filled by fixed-sum grouped nodes
(Lemma 5.1, Prop. 5.2), and certified via a tilted transfer-
matrix cone subtraction (Theorem 4.1) reduced to exact 28-
digit integer 2×2 Perron-root comparisons (Appendix A.4,
Eq. A.6).
Honest Scope Boundary.This companion artifact
independently machine-checks the finite algebraic seeds,
two-color slot axioms, port/score synchronization and
companion-path exclusion mechanics, zero-fiber pooling
and spoke-star geometry, exact rational Eq. (A.1) filling-
price inequalities against Table 2’s floored bounds, The-
orem 4.1 tilted-cone subtractions, and Appendix A.4’s
verbatim Round 9 28-digit integer matrices and 1000th-
power Eq. (A.6) bracket from [ 4]. It doesnotreplay the

P0, P1, P2.
