# Canonical rectangle observations for matrix gradients: fixed weak spaces and checkerboard counterexamples

Working paper - not admitted to the AIRR accepted collection.

We study finite positive matrix-weighted gradients on periodic lattices, with observations restricted to normalized rectangular means in a fixed internal basis. For a spatially constant coherent rank-q weak projector, the minimum actual restoring rank on the flat coexact domain has order q*n^D+g*n^(D-2), uniformly in q. A coefficient-only original-channel rectangle construction attains this order; its least quadratic coefficient has order c*min(g/q,n^2). The proof uses a stated Marcus-Spielman-Srivastava theorem augmented to control Gram matrices and cardinalities, with derived finite Neumann bounds. Spatial constancy is substantive: an exact positive two-channel checkerboard with identical local spectra has full output counts O(n log n) in dimension3 and O(n^2) in dimension4, with finite size-dependent penalties. A plane construction works at every fixed positive angle. Exact historical priority, practical usefulness, efficient implementation and conditioning remain unestablished.
