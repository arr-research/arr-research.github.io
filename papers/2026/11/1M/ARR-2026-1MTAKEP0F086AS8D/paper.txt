Algebraic Lean 4 Lemmas and Exact-Rational Instance
Verification for Five-Agent Envy-Free Cake-Cutting
Lluis Eriksson∗

Abstract
Aris Filos-Ratsikas, Alexandros Hollender, Mohamad Lat-
ifian, and Giannis Tyrovolas (arXiv:2611.33149, Nov.
2026) resolved the polylogarithmic query-complexity prob-
lem for five-agent contiguousε-envy-free cake cutting un-
der monotone Lipschitz valuations, achievingO(log3(1/ε))
value queries and a purely intermediate-value-theorem exis-
tence proof. Their construction parameterizes four retained
pieces P1,P2,P3,P4 plus two potential gapsL,R around
an anchor cut m∈ [0,1]subject to maximal feasibility
(ℓ1 + ℓ2 = m or ℓ3 + ℓ4 = 1 −m), leaving-agent Hall
robustness, softmax weight balancing (Wj = 5/4, non-
favourite cap2−5 = 1/32), quantitative stability (3γ−3δ)
and Lipschitz continuity (13γ−3), and spanning-tree bisec-
tion (O(log3(1/ε))vs. O(log5(1/ε))). We present a compan-
ion verification stack combining two explicitly separated
layers: (i) a standalone, zero-dependencyLean 4.35.0
module (EnvyFreeCake5.lean) formalizing the 18 under-
lying cleared-integer / natural-number algebraic, counting,
pigeonhole, ceiling-margin, and triangle-inequality lem-
mas (0 sorry, 0 warnings, 0Classical.choice; 2 zero-
axiom, 16[propext, Quot.sound]), and (ii) a pure-stdlib
exact-Q instance verifier (EF-CAKE5-CERT-1.0) checking
concrete finite rational witnesses for Hall robustness, soft-
max balance, non-zero stability/Lipschitz perturbations,
and complete 8-edge spanning-tree allocations. Across 16
canonical certificates (0.246–0.934 ms, total7 .80 ms), all
finite invariants replay identically overQ while rejecting
60/60 live adversarial forgeries.
1 Introduction
Contiguous envy-free cake cutting guarantees that every
agent receives a single interval of[0,1]and weakly prefers
her own piece to any other [6, 7]. For monotone 1-Lipschitz
interval valuations, prior polylogarithmic-query algorithms
reached three agents inO(log2(1/ε))queries [2] and four
agents inO(log3(1/ε))queries [4]. Filos-Ratsikas et al. [3]
resolved five agents inO(log3(1/ε))value queries (Theo-
rem 2) via four interlocking pillars:

We delivered a two-layer, zero-floating-point verifica-
tion companion to Filos-Ratsikas et al. [3], combining
18 zero-sorry, zero-Classical.choice Lean 4.35.0 alge-

verifier EF-CAKE5-CERT-1.0, 16 canonical certificates (in-
cluding strictly non-zero Mode 3 perturbation/Lipschitz
witnesses), and 60/60 live adversarial forgery rejections.
