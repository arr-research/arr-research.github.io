# A sharp stationary moment threshold away from equality for Boolean Doob chains with two bases

Lluis Eriksson. Working paper ARR-2026-54GRS2WX6182EVEB, v1.

For Boolean noise of correlation one half, consider reversible Doob transforms whose stationary law factors through a binary linear quotient admitting two disjoint column bases. For every fixed moment order from two up to but excluding 15/7, we construct chains with exact stationary marginal matching through a linear fraction of the coordinates, exponential convergence of the density to one in that moment norm, and an exponentially small spectral gap. A Hamming sphere traps the chain, and a multiplicative correction to its edge law makes the matching exact. Together with the prior sufficient criterion above 15/7 this identifies the threshold away from equality. At the second moment, a uniformly bounded inverse stationary moment of order above 35/9 suffices for ordered spectral recovery at arbitrary rank.

## Scope

The strict upper side of 15/7 was proved in section 7 of working paper ARR-2026-2SH3CGGBHG9Q5BYN, v1. This paper proves the strict lower side, a floor-free exact-matching lemma and a recovery criterion with a bounded inverse moment. Equality without inverse control is open.
