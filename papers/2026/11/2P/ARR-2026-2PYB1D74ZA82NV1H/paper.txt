Abstract
Motivated by the recent proof of AC0 ̸⊆ BQAC0/qpoly via sublinear-degree Pauli approxima-
tion by Xiaoming Sun and Jingheng Wang (arXiv:2611.34803v1), we construct a fully machine-
checked, zero-float verification and certificate suite (PAULI-AC0-BQAC0-CERT-1.0) formalizing
the Pauli-spectrum, Boolean Fourier, twirling-contraction, and stretched-exponential inequali-
ties underlying constant-depth quantum-advice lower bounds. All 20 core theorems are proved
constructively in Lean 4.36.0 (zero sorry, zero Classical.choice, -DwarningAsError=true)
and paired with an AST-audited pure-Q Python verifier emitting 16 SHA-256-indexed ratio-
nal certificates (C01–C16) validated by 62 adversarial tests. Specifically, we certify: (i) exact
n-qubit Pauli weight subspace compression ∑ k≤d (n k ) 3k/4n at sublinear cutoff d = ⌈√n⌉, col-
lapsing from 67/256 ≈ 0.2617 at (n, d) = (4, 2) to 163669/416 ≈ 3.81 × 10−5 at (n, d) = (16, 4);
(ii) exhaustive symplectic verification of Z-group Pauli twirling TZ (O) = 2−n ∑ z Z z OZ z on
n = 2 (16 Paulis) and n = 3 (64 Paulis), proving exact orthogonal projection onto 2n diago-
nal I/Z operators (non-expansive globally and strictly contracting in Frobenius norm whenever
off-diagonal support is nonzero); (iii) exact closed-form Boolean Fourier spectra and Parseval ver-
ification (∑ k Wk = p) for P-uniform linear-size monotone depth-2 CNFs fm,w = ∧m j=1 ∨w i=1 xj,i,
quantifying exact degree-d L2 2 and operator-norm approximation lower bounds across balanced
instances (n = 18: 35.60% variance lost above d = 2; n = 44: 26.98% variance lost above d = 3);
(iv) zero-float Taylor-certified stretched-exponential upper bounds e−mα < (∑K j=0(mα)j /j!)−1
for state 2-design indistinguishability (e−2251/2 < 3.10 × 10−7, e−6251/2 < 10−10) and Anshu–
Breuckmann–Nirkhe NLTS low-energy spectral weight (2e−642/3 < 2.27 × 10−7); and (v) exact
advantage-ceiling transfer from operator-norm Pauli approximations.
1 Introduction
A central question in quantum circuit complexity asks whether constant-depth quantum circuits
with unbounded-fanin gates and polynomial-size quantum advice (BQAC0/qpoly) can simulate clas-
sical constant-depth circuits (AC0) [1, 5]. In a breakthrough preprint, Sun and Wang [7] prove
AC0 ̸⊆ BQAC0/qpoly in the single-copy single-output model: explicit P-uniform linear-size mono-
tone depth-2 CNFs resist every BQAC0/qpoly circuit up to inverse-polynomial worst-case advantage
over random guessing.
The proof engine of [7] combines product-projection localization with Pauli twirling to approx-
imate the circuit’s acceptance operator in operator norm by an advice-independent Hermitian op-
erator of sublinear Pauli degree, reducing quantum-advice hardness to low-degree Boolean Fourier
concentration [4, 6]. The same operator-norm Pauli approximation yields stretched-exponential
e−mα (∀α ∈ (0, 1)) bounds against distinguishing state 2-designs from Haar-random states [3] and
against preparing low-energy states of Anshu–Breuckmann–Nirkhe NLTS Hamiltonians [2] (spectral
weight at most 2e−mα below constant energy density).

Proposition 4.2 (Operator-Norm Advantage Transfer; Lean T18–T20, C15–C16). Let M (0 ⪯ M ⪯
I) be the acceptance POVM element of a circuit and let H≤d be a degree-d Hermitian approximator
satisfying ∥M − H≤d∥op ≤ ε (ε ≥ 0). For any two density matrices ρ0, ρ1 (e.g., input-ensemble state
mixtures for distinguishing or balanced Boolean classification), define the distinguishing advantage
adv(A) := supρ0,ρ1 |Tr(A(ρ0 − ρ1))| over the allowed state pairs. Since ∥ρ0 − ρ1∥1 ≤ 2, Hölder’s
inequality and the triangle inequality give |Tr((M − H≤d)(ρ0 − ρ1))| ≤ 2ε, hence
adv(M ) ≤ adv(H≤d) + 2ε. (5)
As illustrative rational benchmarks of this transfer rule (Table 1), Lean T18–T19 certify adv(M ) ≤
1/20 + 2/100 = 7/100 < 1/2 (C15) and adv(M ) ≤ 1/500 + 2/1000 = 1/250 < 1/2 (C16).
