# Prices and unused volume in Britain's demand flexibility auctions

Lluis Eriksson. Working paper v1.

We reconstruct 620 live half-hour windows of Britain's Demand Flexibility Service in July–September 2026. Among 518 demand-reduction windows, 206 across 40 events had unfilled published requirements and both accepted and rejected positive-volume bids. Every such window contained a whole rejected bid that fit the residual national and zonal volume limits. Yet in every window the highest accepted price was strictly below the lowest rejected price, even before filtering for volume. The smallest price gap was GBP 0.20/MWh. Thus prices alone can reproduce the observed binary decisions within each mixed window, without identifying the operator's actual threshold or the value of additional flexibility. A separate settlement analysis preserves missing and negative values.
