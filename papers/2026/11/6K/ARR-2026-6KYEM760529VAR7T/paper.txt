Abstract
Inspired by OpenAI’s November 24, 2026 preprint em-
bedding planar Euclidean mean width W(S) = P/2,
collinear copy insertion, contraction monotonicity, and
borrowed-wire margins into Strassen’s asymptotic spec-
trum of tensors, we present an explicitly scoped, stan-
dalone, zero-floating-point verification stack pairing two
layers. Layer 1 ( AsymptoticSpectrumGeometry.lean,
Lean 4.35.0; 18 theorems,0 sorry,0 Classical.choice,
0warnings) proves exact2Dinteger Euclidean geome-
try laws—Euclid’s Pythagorean parametrization, trans-
lation/homogeneity invariance, Lagrange’s2DCauchy–
Schwarz, rational-length triangle inequality (withru,rv ≥
0), segment-restricted collinear zero-excess (with0≤t≤k
and r≥ 0), and2Dscaled-rotation (similarity) isometry
and contraction monotonicity—together with elementary
integer and list-sum algebraic projections (distributivity,N
power laws, list-sum monotonicity, and linear margin con-
tradictions). Layer 2 (SPEC-GEOM-CERT-1.0, pure-stdlib
Python Fraction) provides a self-contained four-mode au-
ditor that replays16/16canonical exact-Q certificates in
0.071–0.313 ms (auditing global strict CCW polygon con-
vexity andW(S) = P/2, collinear vs. exterior triangle gaps,
2Dsimilarity contractions, and finite arithmetic barrier in-
equalities), cross-checksW(S) = P/2against4096-angle
Cauchy–Crofton integration, and rejects58/58live adver-
sarial forgeries.
1 Introduction and Scope Calibra-
tion

morphisms F. OpenAI’s November 24, 2026 preprint [1]
