# How precisely do published regressions measure the reallocation of green investment?

Lluis Eriksson. Working paper v1.

After an increase in environmental political risk, how much of the estimated fall in green investment is offset by other investment? The main asset-scaled regressions in Gustafson, Lel and Qin imply a point ratio of 28/37. Three control coefficients violate a necessary accounting restriction by more than rounding can explain. Marginal standard errors alone give a conservative asymptotic 95% interval of [0.020, 2.956], rounded outwards. Green capital expenditure subsequently rebounds. Its two horizon coefficients sum to 0.45 per thousand of assets, but either sign of the population sum is compatible with the marginal evidence.
