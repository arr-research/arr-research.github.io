# Stationary concentration and local return in Boolean Doob chains

Lluis Eriksson. Working paper ARR-2026-2SH3CGGBHG9Q5BYN, v1.

We study finite Boolean Doob transforms at half noise. At an explicit stationary moment endpoint, we give a sufficient tail condition for recovery of the ordered spectrum. Above the cubic moment, under marginal matching and a fixed floor, a killed transition matrix on a joint exceptional set determines the ordered spectral error up to uniform factors and an exponentially small remainder. Without matching, two stationary density values and a bounded cubic moment give a uniform gap. Three exact values already allow exponential trapping with a fixed floor and exponential convergence of the density to one in some supercubic norm. The higher-order matched construction is exponentially close to three levels. A fractional packing of coordinate bases yields a separate floor-free sufficient condition on linear quotients. The unrestricted matched endpoint remains open.
