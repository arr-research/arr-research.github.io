1 Introduction
Alpha-beta pruning [6, 9] is a foundational game-tree
optimization, yet unifying its imperative early cutoffs
with compositional denotational semantics for sequential
games [2, 3, 4] has remained subtle, motivating recent
selection-monad formulations [8, 7]. Escardó and Oliva [5]
resolve this cleanly without mutable state or exceptions
via four linked mechanisms:
1.Monadic Continuation Pruning (Sec. 2–3):Lift-
ing a game tree into the continuation monadKRX =
(X→R ) →R and threading( α,β)bounds via the
Reader monad replaces exhaustivemax/min over chil-
dren with short-circuitingtakeUntil folds that abort
evaluation the moment a maximizer meetsβ or a mini-
mizer meetsα.

mm(T) < β⇒v = mm(T)), yielding Corollaries 1(a)
(wide bounds) and 1(b) (in-window exactness).
3. Optimal Play Extraction & Tie-Breaking (Sec. 5,
Def. 2, Cor. 2):Extending values to pairs(v,π) ∈
Z×Play requires strict tie-preserving comparisons
(pickMax keeps the earlier move on equalityx≥y ,
and pickMin on x≤y ) so pruned subgames returning
a tied cutoff bound cannot displace a genuinely optimal
earlier play.
4. History-Dependent Games & Tic-Tac-Toe
(Sec. 6):Supporting variable-branching history-
dependent move sets yields exact root-to-leaf Tic-Tac-
Toe pruning from9! = 362 ,880full plays to9 ,335

[0,4,1,2,6,3,5,7,8].
