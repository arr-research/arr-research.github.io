# On the Large Davenport Constant of the Finite Heisenberg Group

**Author:** Lluis Eriksson  
**AIRR Identifier:** `ARR-2026-39K6N8BVAF8MG8J9` (v1)  
**Date:** 2026-11-28

## Abstract

Let p be an odd prime and let H_{p^3} = UT_3(F_p) denote the Heisenberg group of order p^3 and exponent p. While the small Davenport constant d(H_{p^3}) = 3p - 3, the Gao constant E(H_{p^3}) = p^3 + 3p - 3, and the Loewy length L(H_{p^3}) = D_ord(H_{p^3}) = 4p - 3 have recently been determined, the large Davenport constant D(H_{p^3}) has remained open for every odd prime p >= 5, with the best general upper bound being Grynkiewicz's D(G) <= d(G) + 2|G'| - 1 = 5p - 4. We prove that for every odd prime p >= 3, 3p - 1 <= D(H_{p^3}) <= 4p - 4 < 4p - 3 = L(H_{p^3}) = D_ord(H_{p^3}), yielding a purely conceptual, computer-free proof that D(H_{27}) = 8 and establishing the strict separation D(H_{p^3}) < L(H_{p^3}) for all odd p (in sharp contrast to M_{27} = C_9 rtimes C_3, where D(M_{27}) = 12 > 11 = L(M_{27})). The lower bound is witnessed by two non-isomorphic extremal minimal product-one families of length 3p - 1, carrying p - 1 and 0 central elements, respectively. For p >= 5, we sharpen the analysis of [3p, 4p - 4] by eliminating all sequences whose abelianization has at least two noncollinear atoms at length >= 3p + 1 and all purely collinear-atom sequences supported on t = 2 or t >= 4 lines at length >= 3p, reducing the remaining gap strictly to two explicit structural obstructions (O1)-(O2). Finally, via a fiber-doubled lifting construction, we prove the lower bounds eta(H_{p^3}) >= 7p - 6 and s(H_{p^3}) >= 8p - 7 for every odd prime p >= 3.
