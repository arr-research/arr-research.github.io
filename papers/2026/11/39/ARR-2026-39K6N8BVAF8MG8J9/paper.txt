ON THE LARGE DAVENPORT CONSTANT OF THE FINITE
HEISENBERG GROUP
LLUIS ERIKSSON
Abstract.Letpbe an odd prime and letH p3 = UT3(Fp) denote the Heisenberg group of order
p3 and exponentp. While the small Davenport constantd(H p3 ) = 3p−3, the Gao constant
E(Hp3 ) =p 3 + 3p−3, and the Loewy lengthL(H p3 ) =D ord(Hp3 ) = 4p−3 have recently been
determined, the large Davenport constantD(H p3 ) has remained open for every odd primep≥5,
with the best general upper bound being Grynkiewicz’sD(G)≤d(G) + 2|G ′| −1 = 5p−4.
We prove that for every odd primep≥3,
3p−1≤D(H p3 )≤4p−4<4p−3 =L(H p3 ) =D ord(Hp3 ),
yielding a purely conceptual, computer-free proof thatD(H 27) = 8 and establishing the strict
separationD(H p3 )< L(Hp3 ) for all oddp(in sharp contrast to the non-abelian groupM 27 =
C9 ⋊C 3, whereD(M 27) = 12>11 =L(M 27)). The lower bound is witnessed by two non-
isomorphic extremal minimal product-one families of length 3p−1, carryingp−1 and 0 central
elements, respectively. Forp≥5, we sharpen the analysis of the interval [3p,4p−4] by eliminating
all sequences whose abelianization has at least two noncollinear atoms at length≥3p+ 1 and
all purely collinear-atom sequences supported ont= 2 ort≥4 lines at length≥3p, reducing
the remaining gap strictly to two explicit structural obstructions (O1)–(O2). Finally, via a fiber-
doubled lifting construction, we prove the lower boundsη(H p3 )≥7p−6 ands(H p3 )≥8p−7
for every odd primep≥3.
1.Introduction and Principal Results
For a finite multiplicative groupG, letF(G) denote the free abelian monoid of finite (un-
ordered) sequences overG, written multiplicatively asS=g 1 ·. . .·gℓ with length|S|=ℓ(for a
subsequenceU|S, multiset subsequence removal is denoted interchangeably byS·U −1 orS\U).
A sequenceSis aproduct-one sequenceif 1∈π(S) :={g σ(1) ···g σ(ℓ) :σ∈S ℓ}. The product-one
sequences form a Krull–type submonoidB(G)⊆ F(G) whose atomsA(B(G)) are theminimal
product-one sequences—those nonemptyS∈ B(G) admitting no factorizationS=S 1 ·S 2 with
nonemptyS 1, S2 ∈ B(G). Two fundamental invariants govern factorization arithmetic overG
[4, 7]: thesmall Davenport constantd(G), the maximum length of a sequence having no nonempty
product-one subsequence, and thelarge Davenport constantD(G) = max{|S|:S∈ A(B(G))}.
Alwaysd(G) + 1≤D(G), with equality wheneverGis abelian; for non-abelian groups, however,
D(G) can exceedd(G) + 1 substantially because an atomS∈ A(B(G)) may contain a proper
product-one subsequence whose complement is not product-one.
Letpbe an odd prime and letH p3 = UT 3(Fp) be the Heisenberg group of orderp 3 and
exponentp. Over the past few years, several zero-sum invariants ofH p3 have been settled.

proof thatd(H p3) = 3p−3 for all odd primesp, and Qu and Wang [9] established the Gao–
Zhuang conjectureE(H p3) =p 3 + 3p−3. On the group-algebra side, Godara, Joshi, and
