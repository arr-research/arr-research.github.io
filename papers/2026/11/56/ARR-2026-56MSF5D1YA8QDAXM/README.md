# Rank defects and spectral stability of Boolean Doob chains

Lluis Eriksson. Working paper ARR-2026-56MSF5D1YA8QDAXM, v1.

For half-correlated Boolean noise, we compare the entire ordered spectrum of a reversible Doob transform with the ambient noise spectrum. Exact matching through order k and a bounded stationary second moment suffice for exponential comparison when four disjoint coordinate families span the stationary Walsh code up to a sublinear rank defect. A persistent discrepancy forces a subcode whose support is small relative to its dimension. The comparison also holds for matched stationary laws within a fixed pointwise relative distance of a structured reference, even when their own Walsh span is the full ambient space. The estimates allow a stationary minimum tending to zero. They concern finite Boolean chains and do not establish the unrestricted stationary moment endpoint.
