## Abstract

Kitzinger, Eisert, and Kunjwal (arXiv:2611.34872v1) proved that multi-qubit bound magic exists for all n >= 3 qubits via the stabilizer-closed convex hulls B_n of outputs of n-qubit PPT Pauli channels, leaving open an exact non-floating-point proof of B_2 = STAB_2, the exact relation between entanglement-breaking, PPT, and magic-breaking Pauli channels, and an analytic bound-magic family for even n >= 4. We resolve all three questions with constructive Lean 4.35.0 formalization (18/18 theorems, 0 sorry, 0 Classical.choice) and an AST-audited zero-float exact rational Python verifier (16/16 SHA-256 certificates). First, via an exact Motzkin-Burger Balinski tangent-cone completeness proof, we show that PPT_2 has exactly 252 extreme rays forming two projective Clifford orbits: 60 stabilizer-basis dephasing channels (in EB_2) and 192 rank-6 channels in the single orbit of the 5-cycle channel E_{C_5} (stabilizer A_5, order 60). Every Orbit-2 channel has non-identity Pauli l_1 norm 5 > 3 (exceeding the universal Cauchy-Schwarz ceiling 2^n - 1 for EB_n) yet satisfies I_4 - E(A_j) >= 0 across all 8 x 192 = 1,536 Hermitian matrices from the 8 Clifford facet orbits (22,320 facets) of STAB_2, proving B_2 = STAB_2 and Conjecture 4 at n=2 unconditionally while establishing EB_2 subsetneq PPT_2 subseteq MB_2. Second, via Z[i] Gaussian-sum phase rigidity, we prove that W_{u,v} with c = 3/(2*sqrt(2)) is a universal magic witness for every odd-k stabilizer pair, yielding exact closed-form depolarizing robustness p_depol(n) > 1 - 2*sqrt(2)/3 for all odd n >= 3, while proving that for every even n = 2m >= 2 the flag states F_{2m}(|phi_+-><phi_+-|) collapse identically into STAB_{2m} as uniform mixtures of all Arf-0 (resp. Arf-1) nondegenerate real quadratic stabilizer states. Third, for every even n >= 4 we construct the S_n-symmetric, full-rank, non-factorizable PPT channel family G_n^(delta) = (1-delta)F_Z^(n) + delta F_n acting on |0^n> + |GHZ_X^n>, proving irreducible bound magic for all 0 < delta < delta_crit(n) with exact universal floor 2(2-sqrt(2))/3 > 3/8.
