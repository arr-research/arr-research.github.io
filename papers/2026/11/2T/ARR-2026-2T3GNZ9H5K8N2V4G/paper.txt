Abstract
In the resource theory of quantum-state texture (QST), the unique free state is the flat
pure state f1 = |f1⟩⟨f1| and free operations are CPTP maps satisfying Λ( f1) = f1. Recently,
de Le´ on (arXiv:2611.34865v1) derived a normal form for QST free operations (Theorem 1),

that the two qubit scalar monotones—geometric texture Tg and texture weight Tw—fail
to characterize convertibility for D≥ 3, leaving higher-dimensional reachability governed
numerically by SDPs.
