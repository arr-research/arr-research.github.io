## Abstract

For an n-party quantum state rho_{A_0...A_{n-1}}, the quantum variation of information d_Q(A_i, A_j) := S(A_i|A_j) + S(A_j|A_i) = 2S(A_i A_j) - S(A_i) - S(A_j) measures the symmetric entanglement cost of bidirectional quantum state merging between subsystems A_i and A_j. Unlike its classical Shannon counterpart, negative quantum conditional entropy allows d_Q to break the triangle inequality even when every pairwise conditional entropy S(A_i|A_j) is nonnegative. Restricting to semimetric projections defines two nested hierarchies inside MET_n: the classical/polymatroid chain CUT_n subseteq SUB_n subseteq VI_n subseteq POLY_n and the quantum chain M_n^{cb,+} subseteq M_n^{q,+} subseteq M_n^q subseteq Q_n := Pi_n(QCONE_n) cap MET_n. We establish the complete finite-party geometry and obstruction theory of these cones through three main theorems backed by exact rational certification and targeted zero-dependency Lean 4 kernel proofs. First, at n <= 6 we determine the exact phase hierarchy: CUT_n = MET_n (n <= 4), CUT_5 subsetneq SUB_5 = MET_5 via K_{2,3} over F_2^2, and CUT_6 subsetneq SUB_6 subseteq VI_6 subsetneq POLY_6 subsetneq M_6^{cb,+} = M_6^{q,+} = MET_6 via exact enumeration of all 296 extreme rays of MET_6 (all realized in CB_6^+), all 1736 extreme rays of POLY_6 = MET_6 cap {90 conjugates of I_{K_{3,3}-e} >= 0}, and a Zhang-Yeung separation on Orbit 9 (kappa = 31/30). Second, via the Segre quadric Q^+(3, F_q), we prove K_{m,m} in SUB_{2m} for all m >= 1 whereas K_{m,m}-e notin POLY_{2m} for all m >= 3, and across all 853 connected 7-vertex graphs isolate Rem #4 (G800) violating both POLY_7 (17/16) and CB_7 (13/12 via a 5-cycle inequality F_4 >= 0) yet embedding isometrically in M_7^{q,+}. Third, we discover two minimal universal quantum obstructions among the 853 graphs—QFail1 (G708, kappa_{Q_7} = 21/20) and QFail2 (G873, kappa_{Q_7} = 41/40)—forbidding isometric quantum realization in any Hilbert space whenever present as an induced submetric. Finally, for K_{m,m}-M (n = 2m), exact Z_2 x S_m certification for m = 3..18 reveals an odd/even parity staircase where kappa_{Q_{2m}} matches kappa_{POLY_{2m}} at every even m yet drops strictly below at every odd m >= 5, matching for m <= 18 the partial sums 1 + sum_{k=3}^{floor((m+1)/2)} 1/(2(k-1)!(k-1)(k-2)) converging to (5-e)/2 (conjectured for all m).
