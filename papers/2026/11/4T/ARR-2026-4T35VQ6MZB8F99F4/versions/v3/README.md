# Moment transfer and spectral bounds for Boolean Doob kernels

Lluis Eriksson. Working paper, version 3.

Section 11 constructs exactly matched Boolean Doob chains where the noise denominator goes to zero at a vertex but every eigenvalue stays exponentially close to its Boolean-noise counterpart. Sections 9–14 also treat weak relaxation, a lower bound on the normalized potential, and two different forms of smoothing. The unrestricted full-gap problem is open.
