Abstract
We audit and mathematically sharpen the analytic endgame and logarithmic scale
calculus of the compact Lean 4.35.0 formalization of the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis
(lsernau/grh-lean[1], based on Alpöge’s proof).

decoupling the left-barrier decay exponentρ>σ from the Cauchy analyticity radiusR>σ ,
tightening the amplitude envelope frome2t to e(α+η)t, optimizing the right-barrier decay
β∈ (0,σ− 1), and scaling the Laplace moment order linearly ask = ⌈κt⌉(instead of
quadratically ask = ⌈t2/9⌉), we obtainE(u) −XE ′(u) ≤e −γt at someu∈ [t,λt]for all
X∈ [0,eαt]and all t>t 0 (with explicit finitet0 depending on the polar threshold and kernel
parameters). In New/Main.lean, this collapses the second scalex1 from super-polynomial

1 Introduction and Audit oflsernau/grh-lean
The recent compact Lean 4.35.0 repositorylsernau/grh-lean (grh-compact/, ≈76,000lines

2t−t 2.

While these loose constants suffice for the qualitative deduction of GRH, they conceal sharp
analytic phenomena closely tied to classical one-sided oscillation and localization problems
