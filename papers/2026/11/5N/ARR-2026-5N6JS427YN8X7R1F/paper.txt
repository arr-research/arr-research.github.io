Abstract
Google Research Cogentic (Nov. 25, 2026) introduced
MaximinKiteNE, a deterministic polynomial-time algorithm
computing an exact rational1/5-approximate Nash equi-
librium (δ = 0) in any[0 ,1]-normalized rational bima-
trix game, breaking the1 /3 + δ barrier. Because that
preprint is accompanied by neither machine-checked for-
malizations nor executable exact-rational verifiers and
the halting proof rests on a delicate chain of exact ra-
tional inequalities—froms-kite best-response mixing and
the7 /4welfare cap to the Stage 4 functional bound
H(z,c1,c2) ≤ −1/3000 <− 2−14—independent formal
verification is essential. We deliver an open, zero-
dependency, zero-floating-point verification stack pairing:
(i) MaximinKiteNash.lean, 18 constructive Lean 4.35.0
theorems (zerosorry, zeroaxiom, zeroClassical.choice,
kernel axioms strictly [propext, Quot.sound]) mecha-
nizing the deterministic algebraic, primal-dual, simplex-
rounding, square-completion, shifted-cubic, and contradic-
tion pillars; and (ii)KITE-NASH-CERT-1.0, a pure-stdlib
Python fractions.Fraction auditor across four modes.
We certify 16 canonical witnesses in0.104–2.564 ms, in-
dependently verify H(z,c1,c2) ≤ −1/1280 ≤ −1/3000
across a13 3 = 2,197-point exact rational grid on[1/4,4]3,
reject 60/60 live adversarial forgeries plus raw IEEE-
754/NaN/duplicate-key tokens, and enforce a 40-file SHA-
256 manifest.
