# How much power did an AI cluster draw at the grid peak? A two-date reconstruction of the Phoenix field trial

Lluis Eriksson. Working paper v1.

We compare two date assignments for the same 400 minute observations of an AI cluster in Phoenix. Every reported power value agrees across the figure workbook and standalone CSV, while every timestamp differs by one day at a fixed UTC offset. Joining each date to its own EIA-930 daily peak gives mean cluster power of 75.8% and 99.0% of a common 20-minute reference. Both conclusions are robust to changing the minute endpoint convention. The comparison measures observed coincidence and leaves physical clock provenance and causal avoided load unresolved.
