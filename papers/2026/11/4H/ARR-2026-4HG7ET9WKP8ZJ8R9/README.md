# When did a rezoning happen? Coverage and event dates in two New York City administrative records

Lluis Eriksson. Working paper v1.

We reconcile New York City project records, adopted zoning-map identifiers and Board of Elections general-election tables. A dated-approval filter nominally spanning 2015–2024 yields 126 projects, all approved in 2021–2024. Among 163 qualifying map keys effective in the preceding six years, 160 match project records without an approval date. An exploratory identifier normalization links 118 recent actions one-to-one. For all 118 the approval and map-effective years coincide. Seventy-one were certified in regular citywide Council election years, while 48 became effective in such years. This paired descriptive difference is conditional on coverage and does not estimate a causal electoral effect.
