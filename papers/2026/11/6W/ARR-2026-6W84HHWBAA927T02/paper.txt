1 Introduction
For over half a century, the multitape Turing-machine complexity ofn-bit integer multiplication
was bracketed between Schönhage–Strassen’sO(nlognlog logn )[ 5] and Harvey–van der Hoeven’s
O(nlogn )[ 2] upper bounds, alongside conditional network-coding lower bounds [1] and classical
arithmetic reductions to squaring, division, and integer square roots [3]. On November 24, 2026,
OpenAI [4] settled the exact logarithmic exponent on tapes at1/2: both integer multiplication and
N×Nbit-matrix transposition (n=N 2) takeΘ(n√logn)steps on multitape Turing machines.

5 Scope Boundaries and Outlook
This work mechanizes the exact algebraic, CRT, spectral-transposition, and cleared-integer entropy-
threshold components of [4] and verifies explicit finite witnesses overZ/Q, rather than formalizing the
full multitape Turing-machine simulator, multidimensional cyclic-factor FFT schedule, or information-
theoretic wire-counting recursion of [4]. Extending TAPE-MUL-CERT-1.0 to audit full multitape
head-trajectory traces and recursive block-transposition schedules is a natural next step.
