5 Conclusion
We delivered an 18-theorem zero-dependency Lean 4
kernel (5 zero-axiom theorems, 13[propext,
Quot.sound], 0Classical.choice) and the
exact-QverifierEUCLIDEAN-DIAM-CERT-1.0for the

chain, and Appendix A.1 factor-two core of Giliberti
and Hajiaghayi [3]. Mechanizing the Gaussian-grid
probabilistic couplings of Sections 6–7 and the
communication-complexity space lower bound of
