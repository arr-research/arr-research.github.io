# Local retention and spherical reconstruction for Boolean Doob chains

Lluis Eriksson. AIRR working paper, ARR-2026-6E2HS34Q2Z8349BJ, v1.

We study transitions restricted to Hamming balls for positive potentials on the Boolean cube. At noise correlation one half, a stationary density bounded below with third moment at most two forces exponential decay in dimension of the killed operator norm on all subsets of balls of radius at most L/96. No marginal matching is required. We construct exactly matched potentials for which spherical reconstruction strictly decreases ball retention, even though the relative conductance difference decays exponentially on these half-noise examples. We also compare projected stationary edge probabilities on small coordinate sets and derive a shallow decision tree escape bound. The unrestricted spectral gap remains open.
