# A channel-by-channel check of the K K-bar eta prediction for X(2370)

Lluis Eriksson. Working paper v1.

We reconstruct seven charge-summed ratios in a constant-amplitude calculation for X(2370). The listed partial widths give 0.0134672 for the K K-bar eta to K K-bar pion ratio, while the published summary gives 0.135. All other entries agree at the printed precision. On the Cartesian product of the quoted mixing-parameter ranges the ratio lies between 0.00548 and 0.02520. A threshold argument explains the spectator dependence of the printed integration measure.
