A channel-by-channel check of the K K̄η prediction for X(2370)


                                          Lluis Eriksson

                                               Abstract


         We reconstruct the seven charge-summed ratios in Wang's calculation for a pseudoscalar
     parent. The listed widths give Γ(K K̄η)/Γ(K K̄π) = 0.0134672, while the summary gives 0.135.
     All six other entries agree at the printed precision, including the normalization. We derive
     the extrema over the quoted mixing-parameter ranges and exhibit a spectator dependence in
     the printed phase-space formula. These checks isolate the channel and x the benchmark for
     a future measurement within this model.
