# How many documentary sources sustain Nvidia's lead in reported AI investments?

Lluis Eriksson. Working paper v1.

In a fixed public AI supply-chain dataset, Nvidia retains its lead in distinct documented investment counterparties after any omission of 37 hostnames. A set of 38 yields a tie with Sequoia Capital at 43 counterparties. A solver-free integer certificate checks the lower bound against every rival. When omissions act on groups of hosts connected by at least two matching dated headlines, the minimum becomes 35 groups. Requiring a single matching dated headline gives 32. Preserving capitalization instead yields an apparent change of leader after three individual omissions between two spellings of Nvidia. These results quantify the sensitivity of a documentary ranking under specified rules of observation.
