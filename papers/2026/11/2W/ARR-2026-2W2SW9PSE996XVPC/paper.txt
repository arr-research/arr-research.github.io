1 Introduction
Strassen’s asymptotic spectrum ∆( S) of tensors [ 2] organizes asymptotic tensor rank, subrank,
and algebraic complexity via the compact Hausdorff space of monotone semiring homomorphisms
f : S →R≥0, within which quantum functionals [ 4] provide a rich continuous family of spectral
points. In a breakthrough preprint, Vrana and Zuiddam [1] introduced A-decomposable semirings
and A-good functions (Section 2), proving that for any two spectral points f0, f1 ∈ ∆ with the
A-cover property, the geometric-average envelope Ft = Ff1−t

of complexk-tensors (Theorem 1.1, Theorem 3.1).
Three sharp quantitative and algebraic gaps remain directly exposed by [1]:
1. Spanning multiplicity and finite-copy reverse monotonicity:To construct g1, . . . , gq ∈G

greedy recurrence uj+1 = ⌊w

(Lemma 3.8)?
2. Polynomial multiplication over positive-characteristic fields:In Section 3.7 (Theorem 3.12
and Lemma 3.16), Vrana and Zuiddam prove log-star-convexity of polynomial multipli-
cation tensors πPm,n only over C, relying on the compact Lie group SU(2) and Haar
measure (Lemma 3.13). In Section 4, they extend matrix multiplication (Theorem 4.8)
and graph/hypergraph tensors (Theorem 4.12, Corollary 4.15) to arbitrary fields using
finite spanning groups (Corollary 4.7, Lemma 4.9), leaving polynomial multiplication in
characteristic p > 0 open. Over which positive-characteristic fields does πPm,n admit
