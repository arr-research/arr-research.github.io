The repair quotient is a witness-line calculation and is correctly not promoted to full-space sufficiency.

The real band dimension `2(2R+1)^d` is valid because real and imaginary coefficients occupy two distinct internal coordinates. It is not a double count of a scalar real Fourier space. `2R<n` prevents aliasing. The arbitrary linear-measurement rank budget follows from the kernel of `(C,V)`; the sector result separately uses `(C,P)`, avoiding the invalid inference that an ambient failure automatically survives restriction.
