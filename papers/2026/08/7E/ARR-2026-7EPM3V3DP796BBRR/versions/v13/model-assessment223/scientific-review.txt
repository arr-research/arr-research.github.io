Detailed mathematical assessment of the new full construction

1. Integer geometry, odd sizes and boundary-touching holes. Recursive floor/ceiling splitting yields globally depth-k intervals of lengths h_k or h_k+1, with h_k=max{1,floor(n/2^k)}. Using that global minimum is essential: replacing it by an individual descendant's shortest side could fail at odd sizes. Every positive ancestor-to-descendant clearance is a union of depth-k intervals and is at least h_k. If all ancestor sides are at least2, every descendant direction is shorter, so each axis has a positive clearance. Expanding the descendant by h_k only on positive-clearance faces produces sides in[2h_k,4h_k]. This includes holes touching some outer faces; absent exterior faces do not create edges. These claims support the collar construction rather than assuming uniform geometry for arbitrary shifted holes. The separate small-node case has only side lengths1 or2 and removes at most a corner singleton. The remaining nonempty hypercube-product graph is connected and has at most b=2^D vertices. n=1, singleton residuals, empty residual suppression and large c are all covered.

2. Collar BV estimate. The interval L1 variance coefficient2r(m-r)/m is at most m/2. Contractive coordinate averaging and telescoping give the rectangle L1 estimate without an extra D. Distinct-normal face slabs overlap with volume ratio at most4 and have relative volume ratio at most2. A root tree of depth at most2 exists because every coordinate has an available face. The mean-transport calculation therefore gives the safe4,12,8 coefficients and the factor12 times the sum of slab L1 deviations. Cover multiplicity at most J=2D gives P_D=2J(1+12J). These are loose but consistent constants.

3. Hole-crossing trace. Averaging along each actual h_k-wide normal fibre bounds the nearest outside value by the fibre average plus fibre variation. Summing the at-most-J faces bounds the added hole-crossing jumps by J(P_D+1) times collar variation. Filling the hole with the root mean creates zero internal-hole variation. Thus C_ext=1+J{2J(1+12J)+1} controls unweighted BV extension on the ancestor. The order of operations matters: no coefficient has been extended or reflected, and no weighted quadratic extension claim is made. The earlier strong-edge extension counterexamples therefore do not attack this argument.

4. Cube isoperimetry and folding. The occupied-fibre/projection argument yields c_iso=D(1-2^(-1/D))>=1/2 for half-volume subsets. A full balanced ancestor rectangle has aspect at most2; folding a cube of the longest side onto it visits the initial product rectangle identically and has at most2 preimages per coordinate for vertices/edges. This establishes an unweighted L^(D/(D-1)) inequality with safe C_full=2^(D+1)/c_iso. Applying it after the BV extension to an indicator on the ring yields relative ring isoperimetry c_Omega=c_iso/(2^(D+2)C_ext). The lower norm estimate for an indicator uses min{|T|,|Omega\T|}, so a median split applies and does not require the hole or ring to have comparable volumes.

5. Power argument and original weights. With alpha=2(D-1)/D, q=2D/(D+2), q'=2D/(D-2), the identities alpha D/(D-1)=2 and(alpha-1)q'=2 are correct. The endpoint-factor degree bound contributes2 alpha D^(1/q'), and truncated positive/negative q-edge energies add to at most the original energy. Holder is applied only afterwards to original retained edges. The critical inverse exponent q/(2-q)=D/2 is correct, and at most D retained edges start at any vertex. The stated G_D=2^(2D+10)D^2 C_ext^2 safely dominates K_Omega^2 D^(2/D); indeed the intermediate derivation only needs a smaller factor proportional to D. The small connected graph path estimate b^2 also lies below G_D. Therefore the regional estimate Var_Omega<=G_D S(Omega)^(2/D)E_a(Omega) is supported in all stated cases.

6. Coefficient-only compressed tree. Heavy means mass>t, not >=t. Terminal heavy nodes are disjoint and L<=S(X)/t. The heavy skeleton has B_h<=L-1 branching nodes and at most2L-1 maximal unary chains, including the possible root-leading chain. A unary increment consists of at most b-1 light children and has mass<=(b-1)t. Greedy closure at cumulative mass>=t gives closed ring mass in[t,bt), because the preceding partial sum was<t. Closed rings are disjoint across chains and charge at most S(X)/t groups. There is at most one nonempty residual per chain. Light children at branching/terminal-heavy internal nodes and heavy singleton terminals are bounded by b(B_h+L). The stated1+(2b+3)S(X)/t group bound is safe. The endpoint heavy subtree is handled separately, so a ring does not double-count its inner node and no vertex is omitted. A heavy singleton can exceed the mass threshold, but its residual variance is exactly zero. Atomic mass therefore causes no unjustified appeal to continuum absolute continuity.

7. Rectangles and retained interfaces. First-coordinate-outside decomposition gives at most2D nonempty integer rectangles per ring, with a unique assignment for every vertex. A cube or singleton needs one. Cauchy gives q_Omega^2<=sum_B q_B^2. This strengthens the observation penalty while keeping the entire ring's internal energy, including edges between its rectangular pieces. Only edges between disjoint regions are discarded, with nonnegative coefficients. This is precisely the distinction needed to avoid the canonical-filament internal-certificate failure. No separate weighted Poincare estimate for every final rectangle is required.

8. Normalization and target conversion. Counting means are |B|^(-1/2)sum_B f. Dividing both norm squares and energy by V=n^D changes them to V^(-1/2)|B|^(-1/2)sum_B f, without changing the rank. This matches page95. With t=(cG_D)^(-D/2)/b and S(X)<=M n^D mu^(-D/2), the estimate mu^(-D/2)<=2^(D/2)(1+mu)^(-D/2) for mu>=1 yields the explicit C_rect. The additive1 is absorbed by the target maximum. Constant fields require K>=c under these normalized means; K=c meets that obligation. Restriction to the actual constraint subspace does not require averaging to preserve divergence. These steps establish the stated original local differentiable observation data globally and hence near zero.
