# Hostile novelty and claim-boundary audit — v4

Date: 2026-08-30

Verdict: **PASS after attribution and scope corrections**. Priority was searched in directly overlapping primary literature through the stated date; absolute priority is not guaranteed.

## Material corrections made during the audit

1. The title was narrowed to **Oracle Gram Resolution and Spherical Saturation Phases** so that “sharp” cannot be mistaken for labeled or minimax estimation complexity.
2. G. W. Stewart's 1984 scaled-SVD/QR asymptotics were identified as a direct antecedent of the deterministic spectral-flag mechanism. The paper now cites Stewart before the theorem, in the priority table, in the discussion, and in the bibliography.
3. The defensible spectral contribution is limited to logistic ordering by distance to the transition hyperplane, identification of the bottom projector, the exact stochastic angle law, and its order-statistic complexity.
4. The angular `log R` discussion was softened: classical sup-norm kernel laws suggest an intrinsic logarithm, but the manuscript does not claim a marked-process lower bound.
5. “Exact global spherical saturation” was replaced by “spherical saturation with exact asymptotic constants.”

## Directly overlapping literature checked

- Stewart (1984), scaled singular-value and QR decompositions: antecedent for vanishing-column flags, not for the logistic score ordering or angle law.
- Cambanis--Huang--Simons (1981): classical spherical/elliptical representation.
- Ostrovskii--Bach (2021), Chardon--Lerasle--Mourtada (2024/2026), Hsu--Mazumdar (2024), and Chen--Mazumdar (2026): logistic Hessian, estimation, and optimization results that bound the empirical and labeled claim boundary.
- Bickel--Rosenblatt (1973) and Gine--Koltchinskii--Sakhanenko (2004): primary kernel sup-norm results supporting, but not proving for this marked process, the predicted angular logarithm.
- Amari--Karakida--Oizumi (2019): prior one-unit Fisher decomposition.

No located primary source contained the combined logistic nearest-hyperplane bottom projector, exact half-normal/Gaussian-inverse angle law, and its explicit iterated-limit complexity. No located primary source contained the paper's exact three-phase constants for a single-neuron spherical Fisher/Hessian profile. These are bounded search statements.

## Claims deliberately not made

- first scaled-SVD spectral flag;
- finite-`r` minimax bottom-eigenspace complexity;
- removal or necessity of the complete shell covering logarithm;
- a new labeled estimator, optimization rate, or end-to-end learning theorem;
- novelty of regular variation, spherical representation, or the `r^-1/r^-3` exponents by themselves.
