# Response to the 2.70/10 referee report

The referee's valid requests were implemented as follows.

- The local Schwarzian bridge is now quantitative, with computable derivative-supremum constants and a direct finite-scale comparison.
- The Gaussian coefficient identity now extends locally to isotropic spherically symmetric inputs through the fourth-moment multiplier `q_X`, with a sixth-moment remainder hypothesis stated explicitly.
- The logistic theory now holds for every real `p>0`, including Gamma/polygamma moments, strict global ratio monotonicity, the small-radius jet through `r^6`, and a refined large-radius expansion.
- A finite-sample theorem now gives explicit radial, tangential, and cross-block errors, a tunable relative Loewner sandwich, radial eigenvalue control, and an eigenspace-angle bound. The logistic corollary gives sufficient sample scaling proportional to `r[d+log(1/delta)]/epsilon^2`.
- Rank and empty-transition-slab obstructions are proved. A matching `Omega(rd)` product lower bound and minimax estimator theorem are not asserted because they remain unproved here.
- Reproducibility now includes pinned dependencies, deterministic hashes, three fixed empirical replays, the interval witness, and exact V5 audit reports.
- Priority wording was narrowed after a primary-literature re-audit. In particular, the manuscript now credits the bilateral Gaussian-logistic Hessian antecedent of Ostrovskii--Bach, the generic fixed-point framework of Fisher et al., Chardon et al. v3, Chen--Mazumdar's `p=1` ordering, and Ojo--Olapade for the generalized-logistic Gamma transform.

Suggestions that would have required unsupported global-network, matching minimax, or product-lower-bound claims were retained as limitations and next-paper problems rather than stated as results.
