# Hostile novelty and claim-boundary audit — v5

Verdict: **PASS with bounded novelty claims**. The search and comparison are evidence for claim calibration, not a proof of absolute priority.

## What is actually new in this manuscript

The paper does not claim that Loewner matrices, Gaussian least-singular-value estimates, Davis--Kahan perturbation, order statistics, or Gaussian-process extrema are new. Its contribution is their proof-complete assembly for the sensitivity matrix of one saturating sigmoid neuron:

- exact Loewner monotonicity witnesses for the logistic derivative;
- the spherical Schwarzian bridge and phase diagram;
- the all-power logistic hierarchy with explicit constants;
- a finite-sample relative Loewner theorem;
- deterministic exterior-power spectral lexicography;
- an exact inverse-Gaussian angle law feeding an explicit joint finite-\(r\), finite-\(n\) bottom-eigenspace guarantee; and
- an intrinsic angular-shell lower bound containing \(\sqrt{R\log R/n}\) in the iterated high-probability limit.

## Literature boundary

The final bibliography separates the ingredients from the synthesis: Loewner/Schwarzian theory, Gaussian extreme singular values and smoothed invertibility, eigenvector perturbation, uniform spacings, kernel-density suprema, Gaussian-process bounds, and single-neuron Fisher/Hessian work. The relation-to-prior-work table states the exact difference for each nearby line.

The manuscript avoids “first”, “unique”, and comparable global-priority language. It says that the cited literature does not appear to provide the combined theorem package; this is explicitly a bounded search statement through 30 August 2026.
