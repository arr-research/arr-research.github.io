# Research and provenance audit

## Classical literature

- Dimca (1986) supplies the equality between dual multiplicity and the total
  Milnor number of the tangent hyperplane section.
- Dimca (1987), Proposition 11.24, supplies the weighted tangent-cone
  hyperplane statement.
- Parusiński (1991) gives broader multiplicity results for dual varieties.
- Dimca–Ilardi (2024) explicitly use the formula for smooth complex
  hypersurfaces and relate nodal tangent sections in general position to
  normal-crossing dual points.

The paper therefore does not claim a new general multiplicity principle. Its
claim is the sharp consequence obtained by combining those classical results
with point-span osculating absorption and an exact equality construction.

## Authorial corpus

- B218 already records finite birational Gauss normalization.
- B219 already constructs arbitrarily large special Gauss fibres with nodal
  hyperplane sections, but states only containment of the prescribed points.
- ARR-2026-2MHNZRRJP49Y9SWP v3 supplies the exact tangent-absorption floor.
- ARR-2026-66Q8M61AA196T8BC v2 supplies the higher-contact proper-span family
  `F=f+yG`.

The present record adds the exact reduced Gauss-fibre argument and the branch,
dual-multiplicity, and tangent-cone calculations. It does not claim the
interpolation/extension architecture itself as new.

## Search boundary

The comparison used accessible primary papers, an authoritative monograph,
the cited recent article, and the author's public repositories. It is not an
exhaustive MathSciNet/zbMATH search, specialist peer review, or priority
certification.
