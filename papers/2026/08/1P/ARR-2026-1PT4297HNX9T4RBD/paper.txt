Trace-zero matrices are commutators in great generality [5]; norm bounds for commutators
and their extremizers form an extensive subject [7, 6]. The classical literature also characterizes
self-commutators of compact operators [8]. These nearby problems must be distinguished from

whereas here both factors are Hermitian, equivalently one minimizes a single self-commutator factor
as in (9). Commutator product formulas, randomized product formulas, Magnus parity, Pauli
semigroups, and strong-coupling Zeno limits are likewise established tools [9, 10, 11, 12, 13]. We
do not claim any one of those tools in isolation. After a targeted search, we have not found in
the cited literature formula(7) for an arbitrary traceless Hermitian3×3target, the associated
triangular action, the sharp action-gap classification and finite-step calibration derived from this
balanced permutation-curvature architecture, or the complete-word/off-block theorem chain tied to
forgotten order. This is a scoped literature statement, not a claim of exhaustive priority.

11 Scope, limitations, and conclusion
The theorem chain is finite-dimensional and uses ideal fixed-block pinchings. Fresh order random-
ization is required at each macro-step. Equation(34) needs both central balance and reversal
symmetry; without them a lower-order coherent term can survive. The polarity in the off-block
theorem is not cosmetic, because reduced pinched words need not be invertible. For0<β <1, the
correct limit isetPQP P, notetQP unless P commutes withQ. The exact finite schedule applies
to the effective tetrahedral twirl, whereas the physical-word construction retains itsO(n−1/2)
approximation andn3/4 action cost. We make no claim about infinite-dimensional unbounded
generators, detector energetics, or optimality over all possible control architectures.
Within that scope, the paper provides three closures. The qutrit target cost is no longer
controlled only by trace norm: the middle eigenvalue has an exact operational price. The tetrahedron
is no longer merely a convenient primitive example: it is the unique equal-norm maximizer, and its
finite twirl admits exact semigroup calibration. Finally, forgotten-order diffusion is no longer only
an effective-holonomy statement: it emerges from complete echoed words with a diamond-norm
rate, a critical leakage exponent, and a transparent physical clock bill.
