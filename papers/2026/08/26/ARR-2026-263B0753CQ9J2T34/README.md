## Scope and limitations

The paper proves exact moment and Laplace ordering within canonical two-level Grassmann fields, a finite sufficient coefficient certificate at fixed rank, complete two-level all-field results for `Gr_C(3,6)` and `Gr_C(4,8)`, and a complete oriented two-level exchange theorem for `Gr_C(2,5)`. It does not classify arbitrary spectra having three or more levels, prove a full fixed-Frobenius optimizer, or derive an unrestricted all-distortion rate--distortion function.
