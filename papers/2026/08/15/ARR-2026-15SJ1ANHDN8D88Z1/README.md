# Bayesian Matroid-Union Bounds for Quantum List Discrimination

- ARR identifier: `ARR-2026-15SJ1ANHDN8D88Z1`
- Version: `v1`
- Record type: `research_paper`
- Author: Lluis Eriksson
- Status: accepted founder-pilot deposit
- Canonical source: `paper.pdf`

The paper proves an all-subset matroid-union hierarchy for quantum list
discrimination with arbitrary priors and transfers it to process testers by
canonical compression.  It also gives exact input-dependent channel results:
an all-depth parallel/adaptive list phase for a laminar dephase--prepare
family, and list/multitime consequences for complete unitary-error ensembles.

The record explicitly separates technical preservation from scientific
certification.  The author is also ARR's founder-editor; no independent peer
review, frontier-model screening, or formal proof is claimed.
