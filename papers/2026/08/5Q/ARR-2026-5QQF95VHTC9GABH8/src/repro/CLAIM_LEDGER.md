# Paper 32 claim ledger

## Proved claims

- For every traceless Hermitian target in dimension at most seven,
  norm-optimal self-commutator rank equals the inertia lower bound.
- Dimension eight is the sharp onset of strict rank excess; the manuscript
  gives the complete stated two-ray phase and an exact integer witness.
- For `F_k` with spectrum `(5^(4k),-4^(5k))`, the unrestricted cost is `29k`
  and the rank-at-most-`5k` cost is `30k`.
- For every `t>=1`, `F_(3t)` has unrestricted cost `87t`, minimum optimal rank
  strictly above `17t` and at most `18t`, and inertia rank `15t`.
- Consequently the additive rank excess is unbounded.

## Explicitly not claimed

- `r_*(F_(3t))=18t`.
- The full intermediate curve `30k-j` for arbitrary `k,j`.
- A classification of optimizer matrices or an extremality theorem for the
  displayed families.
- Peer review, proof-assistant certification, or unrestricted novelty.

Paper 32 absorbs and supersedes the unpublished local Paper 31 package.  It
also preserves the exact Paper 30 family replay needed by the integrated
dimension-eight and dimension-nine claims.
