## Title

All-Field Simple-Bivector Rigidity and Exact Shannon Rate--Distortion for Haar Oriented Two-Planes

## Author

Lluis Eriksson

## Abstract

Let W=x wedge y be the unit simple bivector of a Haar oriented two-plane in R^n. We solve the fixed-norm external-field problem for its linear exponential partition in every dimension. If B has canonical skew coefficients p_j, its partition has an exact positive all-degree expansion in the complete homogeneous polynomials h_k(p_1^2,...,p_m^2). Coefficientwise positivity implies that, at every nonzero field and fixed exterior norm, the unique maximizing orthogonal orbit consists of simple bivectors. The optimal overlap has the elementary density f_n(t)=(n-2)(1-|t|)^(n-3)/2 on [-1,1]. We then determine the unrestricted classical Shannon rate--distortion function of the Haar Plucker source under squared ambient loss. The decoder may use an arbitrary standard-Borel memory and is not restricted to the Grassmann orbit: conditional least squares places its reports in the full Grassmann orbitope, the unit mass-norm ball. For n=3,4,5 we prove continuous activation, a unique positive branch, and no radial reentrance. For every n at least 6, a positive fourth cumulant instead forces a discontinuous directional-information onset and an adjacent time-sharing face. We also obtain the exact high-fidelity constant. The result concerns signed Plucker-coordinate compression, not quantum rate--distortion or Born-probability prediction.
