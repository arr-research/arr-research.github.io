# ARR-2026-19BXX42GM38K6VZ3 v1

This founder-owned ARR pilot candidate preserves Lluis Eriksson's research
paper, *Characteristic-Free Bounds and Sharp Gauss-Fiber Examples for
Point-Span Tangent Absorption*.

The record is scientifically separate from, and explicitly related to,
ARR-2026-2MHNZRRJP49Y9SWP: that companion proves the sharper exact global
binomial floor in characteristic zero, whereas this paper develops
characteristic-free two-branch and growing bounds together with complementary
Gauss-map criteria and examples.
