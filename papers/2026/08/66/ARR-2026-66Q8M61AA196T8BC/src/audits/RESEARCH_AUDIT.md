# Adversarial research audit — successor draft 0.2

Date: 2026-08-24

## Claim under audit

Let `X` be a smooth projective integral `d`-fold over an algebraically closed
field, `H` very ample, `1 <= s <= m`, and `Z` a nonempty finite reduced set.
If the span of the value-evaluation lines of `Z` contains the order-`s`
osculating space at every support, write

`m + 1 = q(s + 1) + r`, with `0 <= r <= s`.

The proposed universal floor is

`B(d,s,m) = q*binom(d+s,d) + (r > 0 ? binom(d+r-1,d) : 0)`.

The draft claims `dim(S_Z), |Z| >= B(d,s,m)` in arbitrary characteristic.

## Proof audit

1. **Intrinsic kernel statement.**  The annihilator of the value span is
   `H^0(I_Z H^m)`.  The annihilator of the sum of the order-`s` osculating
   spaces is `H^0(I_(s+1)Z H^m)`.  Since the latter ideal is contained in the
   former, absorption is equivalent to equality of these kernels.  The draft
   now defines both the value span and the principal-parts targets without
   choosing fiber trivializations.

2. **One-point jets.**  Very ampleness makes the cotangent map of the complete
   `H`-embedding surjective at a smooth point.  After choosing one nonvanishing
   section and `d` sections whose ratios are regular parameters, their degree
   `k` monomials realize every local monomial of order at most `a <= k`.
   This uses neither ordinary derivatives nor division by factorials.

3. **Mixed jets.**  At support `p_i`, the product of separators
   `prod_(j != i) ell_ij^(a_j+1)` is a local unit and vanishes to the required
   order at every other support.  Multiplying an arbitrary order-`a_i` local
   jet by this product has degree
   `a_i + sum_(j != i)(a_j+1) = sum_j(a_j+1)-1`.
   Summing the isolated sections proves simultaneous surjectivity.  Raising
   degree uses one section nonzero at every selected support; algebraic closure
   ensures an infinite field.

4. **Support threshold.**  If the number of supports were below `q` when
   `r=0`, or at most `q` when `r>0`, all full order-`s` blocks would already be
   independent below degree `m`.  Absorption would put a space of dimension
   `n*binom(d+s,d)` inside a value span of dimension at most `n`, impossible
   because `d,s >= 1`.  Thus enough distinct supports exist to select the
   claimed full and residual blocks.

5. **Packing.**  For block weight `w`, the jet length is
   `f(w)=binom(d+w-1,d)`.  Its forward differences are nondecreasing, so a
   fixed total weight is maximized by moving weight toward capped full blocks.
   Positive increments force use of the complete budget `m+1`.  This yields
   exactly `q` full weights `s+1` and one residual weight `r` when nonzero.

6. **Rank conclusion.**  Mixed interpolation in exact degree `m` makes the
   chosen jet-block duals independent.  Absorption puts each block, hence
   their direct sum, inside `S_Z`.  Since `S_Z` is generated by `|Z|` value
   lines, `|Z| >= dim(S_Z) >= B(d,s,m)`.

## Counterexample and quantifier search

- Dropping `s <= m` invalidates the full-rank assertion used for a single
  block; no extension is made.
- Dropping completeness of the `H^m` linear system can remove the separator
  products; no subsystem version is made.
- The successor theorem explicitly assumes order `s`; it makes no assertion
  that first-order absorption does or does not force higher absorption.
- Reducedness is used in the definition of a span generated by distinct value
  lines and in the support counting.  No nonreduced support theorem is made.
- Smoothness supplies regular parameters and the binomial jet length.  No
  singular version is made.
- Algebraic closure is stronger than needed for the elementary argument but
  avoids residue-field bookkeeping.  No theorem over a nonclosed field is
  asserted.
- The jet-ample corollary uses precisely the simultaneous fat-point
  surjectivity in its stated definition. For smaller total weights, one
  auxiliary point of the missing weight reduces the claim to the defining
  case.

No counterexample was found within the stated hypotheses.  The principal
remaining mathematical risk is not a detected logical gap but the possibility
that the floor is already implicit in a broader jet-ampleness statement in the
literature; the draft therefore makes no priority claim.

## Exact checks

`repro/verify_higher_osculating_bounds.py` uses exact integer and rational
arithmetic.  It checks:

- `B(d,1,m)` against the published first-order formula for `d <= 8`,
  `m <= 60`;
- `B(1,s,m)=m+1` for every `1 <= s <= m <= 60`;
- 250 brute-force convex-packing instances;
- monotonicity of the asymptotic coefficient in a finite parameter range;
- four rational-normal-curve value/jet fixtures;
- top-order simplex-lattice fixtures in `(d,m,s)=(2,2,2)` and `(3,3,3)`.

All checks pass, and the saved `repro/results.json` exactly matches fresh
script output.  These checks do not prove the universal theorem, novelty, or
priority.
