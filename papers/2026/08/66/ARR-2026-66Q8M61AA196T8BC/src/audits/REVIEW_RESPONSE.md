# Scientific revision record

This successor is not a cosmetic revision of the earlier tangent-absorption
paper. It isolates a stronger hypothesis and proves a higher-order hierarchy.

## Changes relative to the published first-order paper

1. Replaced tangent absorption by explicit order-`s` osculating absorption;
   the manuscript makes no unproved implication in either direction.
2. Formulated osculating spaces through principal-parts evaluations, avoiding
   ordinary derivatives and factorials in arbitrary characteristic.
3. Proved full one-point jets for powers of a very ample line bundle by local
   parameter monomials.
4. Proved simultaneous interpolation for heterogeneous fat-point orders with
   total weight at most `m+1` by products of point separators.
5. Extracted a mixed-block rank certificate and optimized it by discrete
   convexity to the closed floor `B(d,s,m)`.
6. Audited the support-count threshold separately for zero and nonzero
   Euclidean remainder.
7. Recovered the published growing first-order bound exactly at `s=1` and
   replaced the citation-dependent combined corollary by a self-contained
   extension to arbitrary jet-ample polarizations.
8. Proved exactness for every order on rational normal curves and in the
   top-order regime `s=m` on projective space in every dimension.
9. Added exact rational/Hasse-jet fixtures and brute-force packing checks, with
   explicit scope limitations.
10. Restricted novelty language: the literature comparison is selective, B215
    is identified as an authorial antecedent, and no exhaustive priority claim
    is made.
11. Following independent Codex review, separated the boundary case
    `q=1,r=0`, required nonempty support lists in interpolation statements,
    defined the packing objective at weight zero, and removed an unsupported
    assertion about tangent versus higher absorption.

## Current gate

Independent Codex review found no critical mathematical defect but requested
the corrections listed above. Fresh compilation, rendered-page inspection,
focused re-review, and final release packaging remain required before ARR
publication.
