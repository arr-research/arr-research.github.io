**Title:** Two-Query Chirality in Tetrahedral Quantum Echoes: Exact Parallel Readout, a Nine-Dimensional Query Defect, and a Certified Adaptive Advantage

**Author:** Lluis Eriksson

**Primary area:** Quantum information / quantum channel discrimination

**Secondary area:** Mathematical physics

**Keywords:** quantum channel discrimination; adaptive quantum strategies; quantum combs; tetrahedral symmetry; unitary echoes

**Length:** 12 pages, 1 figure, 3 tables

**Abstract:** Four balanced Pauli kicks along the vertices of a regular tetrahedron, followed by their reverse echo, generate 24 order-labelled qubit unitary channels. All words have the same trace and split into two 12-element orbits of the proper tetrahedral group. One query sees only the common trace. At two queries, exact chiral energies enter a scalar-clamp solution of the Bell-square and global parallel problems. At the algebraic point q=1/2, every causal two-query protocol has success at most 9/24, while an explicit rational two-comb normalization and exact weighted-frame identity attain this cap. Hence the optimal causal success is 3/8 and exceeds the global parallel value (5+sqrt(15))/24 by exactly (4-sqrt(15))/24. A six-Kraus realization makes the adaptive circuit explicit, and a uniform Lipschitz bound proves strict advantage on a certified open pulse interval. The separation is analytic and verified by quotient-ring arithmetic, with no numerical SDP. No claim is made about indefinite causal order or noisy hardware.
