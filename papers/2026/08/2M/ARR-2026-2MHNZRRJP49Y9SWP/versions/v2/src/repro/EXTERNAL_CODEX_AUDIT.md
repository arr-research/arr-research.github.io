# Independent Codex referee report

Date: 2026-08-24

Manuscript: working version 0.7
Status: independent model review; not human peer review

## Verdict

The referee found no P0 defect and no counterexample satisfying all stated
hypotheses. The principal theorem, perfect-field fattening lemma, adapted
projection, transfer argument, complex sharpness construction, and secondary
rank/Gauss refinements were judged mathematically correct.

Score before author response: **7.6/10 with uncertainty +/-0.8**.

Publication recommendation before response: mathematically publishable after
moderate revision, with bibliographic positioning and several exposition
points still to close.

## P1 findings and response

1. The Frobenius/`p`-th-root mechanism was already explicit in
   Bocci--Chiantini, Remark 2.2, and could not be presented as a new argument.
   **Resolved:** the abstract, literature comparison, novelty paragraph and
   provenance now attribute that argument explicitly and claim novelty only
   for its combination with the adapted projection and exact absorption floor.

2. Priority of the combined theorem is not externally certified.
   **Delimited, not “resolved”:** the paper says that its targeted search is
   not a priority certification, makes no claim of exhaustive coverage, and
   reports the public-corpus search only as negative evidence.

## P2 findings and response

- The unnecessary etale/flatness assertion in the projection proof was
  removed. The lemma now states exactly the finite, injective and tangent-map
  properties used later.
- The Bertini paragraph now identifies the smooth complement and the
  base-point-free restricted system before applying characteristic-zero smooth
  Bertini.
- The quadric sentence now proves geometric-point injectivity from the polar
  radical and explicitly treats the characteristic-two inseparable caveat.
- The convention `N={0,1,2,...}` is explicit.
- “Exact inputs” was weakened to “inputs used by this proof.”

## Focused re-review

The same independent referee re-read the revised passages. It confirmed that
the attribution, Bertini formulation, characteristic-two quadric argument,
environment balance, labels and eleven bibliography entries were sound. Its
only residual P2 was the phrase “Etaleness transfers,” left in the introduction
after the projection lemma had been narrowed. That phrase has now been replaced
by the exact cotangent-isomorphism statement used in the proof.

Updated score: **8.4/10 with uncertainty +/-0.6**.  Updated verdict:
mathematically publishable, subject to the explicitly retained uncertainty
about bibliographic priority.

## Scope of the assessment

The referee reran all three replay families and confirmed byte-for-byte
agreement with the committed JSON. The assessment does not certify universal
truth, novelty, priority, or acceptance by an external journal.
