# Research and provenance audit

## Classical literature

- Aluffi–Cukierman (1993) provides the scheme-theoretic jet-incidence setting;
  the local Tjurina-algebra identity here is presented as its hypersurface
  specialization/immediate calculation, not a new general discriminant theorem.
- Dimca (1986) supplies the dual multiplicity–Milnor formula; Parusiński (1991)
  provides broader multiplicity results.
- Saito (1971) supplies the criterion `mu=tau` iff the isolated hypersurface
  germ is analytically quasihomogeneous.
- Greuel–Lossen–Shustin (2007), Corollary 2.24, supplies finite contact
  determinacy; Section 2.2 is cited for contact equivalence.
- Piene (1978) contextualizes the classical Hessian description of Gauss
  ramification, and Tevelev (2005) supplies projective-duality context.
- Ballico–Chiantini (2021), Theorem 3.5, is cited only as a comparison for a
  squared-point independence criterion, not as the arbitrary-jet lemma proved
  in the paper.

## Authorial corpus

The three related ARR records supply the tangent and higher-osculating support
floors and the preceding dual-multiplicity construction. They are explicitly
labelled authorial antecedents, not independent validation. A public-corpus
scan found no prior authorial statement of the full Tjurina-length/Fitting
identity plus the simultaneous exact defect ladder deposited here.

## Claim boundary

The local identities are classical or immediate specializations. The claimed
contribution is the exact combination of absorption-forced scheme-fibre and
ramification bounds with an explicit simultaneous Tjurina–Milnor defect ladder
at a stated sufficient degree. No exhaustive priority, minimal-degree, sharp
Tjurina-floor, human-review, or formal-verification claim is made.

## Search boundary

The comparison used accessible primary papers, an authoritative monograph,
the cited recent article, related ARR records, and the author's public corpus.
It is not an exhaustive MathSciNet/zbMATH search, specialist peer review, or
priority certification.
