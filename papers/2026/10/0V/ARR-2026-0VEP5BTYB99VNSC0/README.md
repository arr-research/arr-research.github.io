# Six-spin Lorentzian obstructions for clique blowups of independence-stable graphs

Let F be a finite connected simple graph with at least one edge such that deleting any edge leaves its independence number unchanged. For every integer s at least two, we prove that the clique blowup F[K_s] admits a strictly positive rational rank-two antiferromagnetic target on six states whose graph partition polynomial is not Lorentzian. The proof classifies minimum-induced-edge subsets using equality in Turan's theorem, identifies an exact four-profile block of inertia (2,2,0), and compresses all higher profiles by a scaled generalized Vandermonde matrix. This yields obstructions for connected biregular bipartite bases other than K2 and for connected bases with a unique maximum independent set and at least one edge. Exact computations check 994 base graphs, 720 stable blowups and four positive rational six-state Hessian witnesses. Six is an upper bound; optimality, uniform target parameters and the general connected-source characterization remain open.
