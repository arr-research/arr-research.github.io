# Finite convex interpolation for polynomial Lyapunov existence

We characterize polynomial Lyapunov existence over all degrees for planar homogeneous polynomial fields with nonvanishing angular velocity. A moment threshold separates strict polynomial certificates from the absence of local analytic weak certificates in the stable case. Classical confluent Nevanlinna-Pick interpolation gives a finite convex semialgebraic formulation, with exact boundary decision for algebraic coefficients. For each odd field degree, the certificate region is open and semialgebraic, and compact admissible families have a computable common certificate degree. For rational fields in dense binary encoding, we prove polynomial-time additive approximation of the threshold and polynomial-time decision under a supplied positive gap promise. Explicit inner and outer radii permit a classical weak convex optimization reduction; a uniform functional perturbation argument handles algebraic poles without constructing their common number field. This approximates a value and does not bound the output size of a Lyapunov polynomial polynomially. Jackson bounds and a constructive sufficient-degree estimate complement the finite formulation. An exact cubic example has an algebraic threshold and minimum Lyapunov degree eight at one rational parameter. Beyond the rotating class, a boundary-factor obstruction produces a full twelve-dimensional rational box of stable quintic fields with sign-changing angular velocity and no local analytic weak certificate. One explicit rational C1 Lyapunov function works for the entire box. The latter result is a sufficient obstruction, not a general classification with angular zeros. General certified solver implementations are not supplied.
