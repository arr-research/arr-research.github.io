# Linear Fisher information from a probe coupled to an inaccessible quantum memory

A finite quantum probe repeatedly interacts with an inaccessible memory. Three sufficient conditions establish classical Fisher information proportional to the number of recorded prepare-and-measure rounds. The result is local and conditional and does not establish universal optimality or quantum advantage.
