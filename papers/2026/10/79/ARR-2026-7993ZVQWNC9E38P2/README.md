# Rank-two obstructions to Lorentzian graph partition functions

We give rank-two antiferromagnetic obstructions to the Lorentzian property of graph partition functions. A connected noncomplete source of maximum degree Delta has a strictly positive rational obstruction on Delta+1 spins whenever no two maximum-degree vertices have equal closed neighbourhoods. This covers graphs without true twins, unique maximum-degree vertices and connected noncomplete edge-regular graphs. Conditioned-set Hessian kernels also yield a neighbourhood-independence-gap criterion. One fixed nonnegative four-spin target characterizes cliques among connected diamond-free and complete multipartite sources; a fixed five-spin target does so for joins of a nonempty clique with disjoint nonempty cliques. Positive perturbations may depend on the source. Exact certificates cover all 989 connected noncliques of orders three through seven. An explicit ten-vertex graph shows that the small-parameter criterion is incomplete. The connected-source conjecture and completeness of the full hierarchy remain open.
