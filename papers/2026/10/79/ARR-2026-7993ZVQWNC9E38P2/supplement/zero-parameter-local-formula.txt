EXACT LOCAL-TYPE FORMULA FOR T_h
Author-side note, 17 October 2026.

For each ordered edge (u,v), remove u,v and divide the other n-2 vertices
into four classes. Their sizes are
  c = |N(u) intersect N(v)|,
  a = d_u-1-c,
  b = d_v-1-c,
  d = n-d_u-d_v+c.
These classes consist of common neighbours, neighbours only of u,
neighbours only of v, and neighbours of neither endpoint.

An h-set S has r neighbours of u and t neighbours of v precisely when
it chooses k common neighbours, r-k only-u vertices, t-k only-v vertices,
and h-r-t+k neither-endpoint vertices. Therefore the contribution of
this ordered edge to (T_h)_(r,t) is

sum_k binom(c,k) binom(a,r-k) binom(b,t-k) binom(d,h-r-t+k).

A binomial coefficient is zero outside 0<=lower<=upper. Summing over all
ordered edges proves the formula, with no independence restriction on S.
The script groups unordered edges by (min(d_u,d_v),max(d_u,d_v),c), and
adds both orientations. Diagonal entries consequently receive both
orientations, as required by the manuscript convention.

The formula is exact at arbitrary n and depends only on these edge types.
It does not imply that T_h must be indefinite. C5[K2] is an explicit
counterexample to that proposed universal criterion: every T_h, h=1,...,8,
is positive semidefinite. Exact matrices and rational congruence blocks
are stored in blowup-layer-checks.json; direct subset enumeration agrees
with the local formula on all eight levels. The same graph does have
positive rational rank-two obstructions from the full hierarchy, directly
verified at h=3,4,5 and ell=-99/100. Thus this is a limit of the sufficient
zero-parameter test, not a counterexample to the source conjecture.
