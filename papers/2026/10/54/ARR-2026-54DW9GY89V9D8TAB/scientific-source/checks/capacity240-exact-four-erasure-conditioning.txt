1. Joint spectrum and real span
U,V commute, have order3, and their labels generate Z3^2. Every nontrivial

dimensionn by Fourier inversion. Thus H in the real Hermitian span ofE

Hence ||H||_F^2=3n(sum f_i^2+sum g_j^2).

Every nonzero Hermitian trace-zero D with n_+(D),n_-(D)<=r is a positive
scalar multiple of a difference of two allowed states: normalize its PSD
spectral parts by their common positive trace. Conversely each state
difference has those two inertia bounds. The ratio of norms is homogeneous.
Consequently the squared maximum fraction of energy in P_E is
 Lambda_* = sup_D ||P_E D||_F^2/||D||_F^2,
over that inertia/trace cone. If Lambda_*<1 then C=(1-Lambda_*)^(-1/2).

2. Dual upper bound valid for all such D, including coherences

The trace rearrangement inequality gives

Hilbert-space duality on the real Hermitian span gives

Therefore Lambda_* <= sup_H W_r(H)/||H||_F^2.
We will attain this upper bound with genuine state differences below.
