ROOT269 — EXACT ONE-SIDED LOCAL-ALGEBRA ERASURE AND CHANNEL COMPLETION
New proposed universal theorem,24October2026. Not in263/248, no grade or
priority certification. All following arguments require separate audit.

Setup: H=C^m tensor C^k, integers m>=1,k>=2. Let K be the real space
{I_m tensor B : B=B*,TrB=0}. P is Frobenius-orthogonal projection ontoK,
Q=I-P. For a trace-zero HermitianD,
 PD=(I_m/m) tensor Tr_A D, ||PD||F²=||Tr_A D||F²/m.
For generalD, replace Tr_A D by Tr_A D-(TrD/k)I_k. Thus Q preserves
the total trace and all input-nonidentity operators, but erases the
output-only traceless local algebra, of real dimensionk²-1.

Proposed sharp constants for integer1<=r<m:
 TWO rank<=r states: C_bal(m,r)=(1-r/m)^(-1/2).
 ONE rank<=r state, other any density state:
 C_one(m,r)=sqrt((m+r)/(m-r)).
Both are exact and attained. For r>=m both uniform inverse constants
are infinite. For m=1 there is no finite positive-rank range. These
constants are independent of output dimensionk>=2.

Universal cone proof for the one-sided upper bound.
Let D=D+-D- be nonzero Hermitian trace-zero, TrD+=TrD-=tau>0, and
n_+(D)<=r (or change sign if n_-(D)<=r). Put a²=||D+||F²,b²=||D-||F².
Then a²>=tau²/r. Let R=Tr_A D+,S=Tr_A D-. Both are PSD of trace tau.
Since TrRS>=0,
 ||R-S||F²<=||R||F²+||S||F²
            <=tau²+min(tau²,m b²).
The second term uses BOTH PSD trace control ||S||F²<=tau² and the
standard partial-trace Frobenius bound ||Tr_A D-||F²<=m||D-||F².
The latter follows by Cauchy--Schwarz in a product basis or by the
adjoint map B->I_m tensor B, whose Frobenius operator norm is sqrtm.
Thus, writing t=b²/tau²>=0,
 ||PD||F²/||D||F²
 <= [1/m+min(1/m,t)]/[1/r+t]
 <= 2r/(m+r).
Indeed if t<=1/m, the fraction(1/m+t)/(1/r+t) is increasing forr<m;
if t>=1/m, (2/m)/(1/r+t) is decreasing. The maximum is at t=1/m,
where it equals2r/(m+r). No commutation assumption is used, and no
total rank bound is imposed on D-. Pythagoras gives C_one as stated.
This argument is stronger than using only a one-sided spectral margin.

One-sided sharpness: choose orthogonal output unit vectorsu,v and any
rank-r input projectorR0, r<=m. Take
 rho=(R0/r) tensor |u><u|,
 sigma=(I_m/m) tensor |v><v|.
They have ranksr,m and orthogonal supports. Their difference has norm²
1/r+1/m, and its projected squared norm is2/m. The retained squared
norm is1/r-1/m. The ratio is sqrt((m+r)/(m-r)) forr<m. At r=m the
states collide exactly, proving failure forr>=m.

Balanced upper bound: if both inertias<=r then a²,b²>=tau²/r, so
||R-S||F²<=2tau²<=r(a²+b²), whence projected fraction<=r/m.
Attain with rho=(R0/r) tensor|u><u| and
sigma=(R0/r) tensor|v><v|. Both states have rankr, norm difference²2/r
and projected squared norm2/m. Thus C_bal is sharp. At r=m they are
also the preceding indistinguishable pair. The state-difference/inertia
reduction is the standard positive/negative spectral-part normalization.

Approximate-rank deterministic consequence.
For ANY densityrho, let tau_r(rho) be the sum of eigenvalues after the
r largest ones, r<m. Let rho_r be the top-r spectral truncation divided
by its trace. If eta=||rho-rho_r||F, then eta<=sqrt2 tau_r: the top
eigenvalue error has squared sum<=tau_r², as does the tail. For any
other state sigma, universal one-sided stability with rho_r gives
 ||sigma-rho||F <= C_one||Q(sigma-rho)||F
                       +(C_one+1)sqrt2 tau_r(rho).
For data y=Qrho+e, ||e||F<=epsilon, ANY minimizer of the convex residual
over all density states exists and obeys
 ||rhohat-rho||F<=2 C_one epsilon
                       +sqrt2(C_one+1)tau_r(rho).
The same argument holds if the minimization domain is any compact convex
subset containing the true rho, in particular states of a fixed marginal.
The truncation rho_r need NOT lie in that constrained domain: the
one-sided state inequality applies against every PSD trace-one competitor.
No low-rank condition on the minimizer and no approximate-rank promise
enforced by the algorithm are required.

Channel interpretation, with conventions made explicit.
Let Phi:M_m->M_k be CPTP and J_Phi=(id tensorPhi)(|Omega><Omega|),
|Omega>=sum_i|i,i>/sqrtm. J is trace-one PSD, Tr_output J=I_m/m, and
rankJ equals minimum Kraus rank. Let F_i,G_j be real Hermitian traceless
orthonormal bases of input/output matrix spaces. Set
 T(Phi)_ji=Tr[G_j Phi(F_i)].
For two TPchannels,
 ||Q(J_Phi-J_Psi)||F=||T(Phi)-T(Psi)||F/m,
because their input-only and identity coefficients agree and
Tr[J_Phi(F_i^T tensorG_j)]=Tr[G_jPhi(F_i)]/m.
Thus for rankJ_Phi<=r<m and ANY CPTP competitorPsi,
 ||J_Phi-J_Psi||F<= C_one(m,r)||T(Phi)-T(Psi)||F/m.
The matrixT is the linear action on traceless inputs. The unknown
translation Phi(I_m/m) is omitted; for equal dimensions this is the
information in the conventional unital part. No assumption Phi unital.

Exact identification threshold: if two TPmaps agree on all traceless
inputs then their normalized Choi difference is(I_m/m)tensorB, with
B tracelessHermitian. If B!=0 its positive and negative inertias are
each>=m. A true Choi matrix of rankr<m cannot have that difference
against any PSDcompetitor. Conversely pure-output replacer channels
Phi_u(X)=TrX|u><u| and Phi_v(X)=TrX|v><v| have identicalT=0 and
Kraus rankm each, so the uniform thresholdr<m is sharp. The CONSTANT
above is an upper bound for the CPTP-restricted domain, not claimed
optimal there: the state attainers need not have marginalI_m/m.
When r=1,m=k, truechannels are unitary, all with zero translation;
do not assert the general state attainer is an extremizing channel.

with Tobs=T(Phi)+N, ||N||F<=delta. The Choi feasible set is compact,
convex and containsJ_Phi. Augment the data with the known input marginal
and trace, and divide correlation coordinates bym. The noise budget
for theQdata isepsilon=delta/m. Therefore for anyr<m,
 ||Jhat-J_Phi||F <=2 C_one(m,r)delta/m
                      +sqrt2(C_one(m,r)+1)tau_r(J_Phi).
