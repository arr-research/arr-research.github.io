Sharp positivity orders for reduction maps
                            on perfect-tensor codes
                                              Lluis Eriksson

                                             25 October 2026
                                       Research manuscript, draft 2



                                                 Abstract
          We classify positivity of tensor products of reduction maps after restriction to perfect-tensor
      codes. For encodings from AME(4, d), the restricted Choi matrix splits into an isotropic code-
      output block and a completely positive complement. This gives the exact rank-constrained
      minimum for every 0 ≤ t ≤ 1. For every AME(6, d), an operator bound obtained from five
      incident-error stars proves an exact simple least Choi eigenvalue and a monotone quintic
      complete-positivity threshold. At t = 1/2, its complementary output block is also completely
      positive, giving an exact positivity-order classification. Uniformly in the code dimension, the
      encoded three- and five-copy maps are respectively 8-positive and 32-positive. These uniform
      orders cannot be increased to 9 and 33, as shown by exact code-projector witnesses. The entire
      column support must lie in the code; the opposite support is free. The results do not imply
      positivity of unrestricted physical tensor powers or allow tensorization of merely k-positive
      maps. We also separate a classical low-rank PPT explanation of qutrit entanglement breaking
      from an explicit Weyl measure-and-prepare realization.


1    Introduction
Reduction-type maps and their tensor powers connect positive-map theory, Schmidt rank and
Werner distillation. We consider

                Φt (X) = Tr(X)Id − tX,            ΛV,t (X) = Φ⊗n     ∗

where V : Cd → (Cd )⊗n is an encoding obtained from an absolutely maximally entangled state on
n + 1 parties. The restriction is on a whole quantum-code support. It is not a statement about
arbitrary physical input subspaces or a plane containing only one code vector.
    The code-output block has a particularly simple form: after the encoding isometry, it is the Choi
matrix of a depolarizing-type map. The other output block is completely positive throughout the
three-copy parameter interval, and at the five-copy Werner endpoint. This operator decomposition
lets the standard isotropic Schmidt criterion control all admissible coefficient matrices, not just
operators supported on the code on both sides.
    For three copies we obtain an exact constrained minimum and all parameter thresholds. For
five copies, pair-error subspaces need not be mutually orthogonal. We retain their possible overlaps
and bound their sum by five incident stars. This proves a universal least eigenvalue without
asserting a universal full spectrum. At the half parameter, a positive complement then yields the
exact rank-positivity test. The uniform orders 8 and 32 are optimal over dimensions for these
encoded families.

Prior ingredients and attribution. Quantum secret-sharing codes and their relation to
perfect tensors are prior [1, 2, 10]. Products of the parameterized maps Tr(X)I − tX already occur
in generalized state inversion [3]. Error-space orthogonality, padded partial traces, AME purities

6    The qutrit EB conclusion and its classical explanation
The abstract entanglement-breaking conclusion has a classical route and should be credited
accordingly. For any AME(4,3), (5) at t = 1 gives J1 = E, of rank 24. It is PPT: Φ1 ◦ T is CP,
with Choi I − F ≥ 0, and ΛV,1 ◦ T = (Φ1 ◦ T )⊗3 ◦ AdV . Its marginals are Trout J1 = 8I3 and
TrR J1 = I27 − V V ∗ . The latter follows by expanding the known reductions of the code projector.
Thus its minimal support is 3 × 24, with rank 24. The low-rank PPT theorem of Horodecki,
Lewenstein, Vidal and Cirac [12] gives separability, hence EB [6].

7    Verification and scope
The supplement contains author exact-arithmetic transcription checks, not a proof-assistant
formalization. Complete three-copy coefficient Grams of dimensions 81 and 625 are constructed
directly from partial traces. The five-copy wheel check constructs all six polynomial coefficients
in dimension 64, the incident-error stars and integer cut determinants. A qubit diagnostic has
T spectrum 0(19), 2(45); disjoint error-pair spaces are not assumed orthogonal. The proof for
arbitrary perfect tensors is the operator argument above, not extrapolation from those finite
controls.

Complex encodings are included in the checks. The coefficient Gram is J, and the corresponding
code projection is W . A non-real three-copy control gives a Gram entry −i/24 whereas omitting
the conjugation gives i/24. Scalar scripts verify the rank-boundary identities and exact negative
values. They do not construct matrices of dimension 2456 ; witness existence and expectation are
analytic. Earlier failed auxiliary multiplicity guesses and symbolic-equality checks are preserved
as source snapshots and failure-summary records, with their provenance stated.
    The entire column or row support must lie in the code. The results do not establish global
rank-two Werner positivity, a theorem for a plane containing just one code ray, a five-copy
all-parameter rank classification, or tensor stability outside CP. Standard codes, QEC privacy,
isotropic optimization and shadow tools are attributed prior ingredients. The source comparison
is bounded; no worldwide originality or operational advantage is certified.
