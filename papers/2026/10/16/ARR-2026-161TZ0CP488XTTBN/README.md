# Sparse Weyl operators: a uniform binary rank theorem and sharp erasure thresholds

Lluis Eriksson · ARR-2026-161TZ0CP488XTTBN · v1 · Working paper.

Canonical PDF: paper.pdf, 12 pages. SHA-256: c3596a65172969836e0049ad26d3932932457c0f7866977a2e8c4b63d8296e88.

The main theorem proves rank at least d/4 for all nonzero complex combinations of at most seven cyclic Weyl operators in every binary dimension d=2^k>=4. The threshold is attained under budgets four through seven. Exact universal erasure budgets follow in the stated complex low-rank classes. Density matrices, tensor-qubit Pauli bases and efficient recovery algorithms are outside the result. The full prime-power conjecture remains open.
