 \begin{abstract}
 We determine the complete equal-reference contraction coefficients of the minimal quantum Fisher metric, its quadratic chi-square divergence and the finite triangular Petz divergence for every unital qubit channel. For a faithful normalized Choi state $J$, all three equal $1-16/\Tr(J^{-1})$; for singular $J$ they equal one. An explicit qubit--qutrit lower family matches a harmonic-weight upper bound and gives the exact stabilization envelope, including a maximal factor of nine between coefficient suprema. For depolarization in dimension $d$, a commuting Bell family matches the order bound $\lambda^2\kappa/[1+\lambda(\kappa-1)]$ for each normalized nonaffine operator-convex Petz generator when $1<\kappa\le d^2/2$. A rank-two ladder extends SLD and triangular sharpness to $\kappa\le d(d+1)/2$, with the budget taken against the actual reference marginal. In the qubit case we identify exact classical-reference and separable/PPT restrictions over the full physical noise range. A localized rational counterexample addresses one printed Doeblin equivalence. These results do not determine unrestricted BKM, relative-entropy, actual-mutual-information or capacity coefficients; the lower constructions are not unitary phase models. Faithful limits, adverse controls, prior-work dependencies and model-review limits are explicit.
 \end{abstract}
