\section{A localized Doeblin counterexample}\label{sec:doeblin}
We record a discrepancy in a specific printed statement, rather than infer a failure of the surrounding theory. In the convention of George et al.~\cite[Definition~4, Eq.~(3.1)]{Doeblin}, the ordinary quantum Doeblin coefficient has the primal form
\[
 \alpha(\N)=\sup\{\Tr X:\ X=X^\dagger,\ I_A\otimes X\preceq\Gamma_{AB}^{\N}\},
\]
where \(\Gamma\) is the unnormalized Choi matrix. Its dual in Proposition~3 admits \(Y\succeq0\) with \(\Tr_A Y=I_B\), minimizing \(\Tr\Gamma Y\). Requiring \(X\succeq0\) instead defines the different coefficient \(\alpha_+\) of Definition~7, Eq.~(3.25); the two optimization problems must not be identified.

Consider \(\N=(\id+\Ad_X+\Ad_Y)/3\). Its Bloch matrix is \(\operatorname{diag}(1,1,-1)/3\), so its \emph{ordinary trace-distance} contraction coefficient is exactly \(1/3\). Direct calculation gives
\[
 \Gamma=\frac23I_4-\frac13vv^*,\qquad v=(1,0,0,-1)^T.
\]
The primal \(X=0\) is feasible. The dual \(Y=vv^*\) is positive, satisfies \(\Tr_A Y=I_2\), and gives \(\Tr\Gamma Y=0\). Weak duality therefore already proves \(\alpha(\N)=0\).
This contradicts the equivalence between positive Doeblin coefficient and strict ordinary trace contraction in Theorem~5 as printed in arXiv:2503.22823v3 and the checked journal PDF. It does not contradict the usual one-sided Doeblin upper bound or turn ordinary trace contraction into the complete SLD coefficient. The four-dimensional primal/dual certificate is logically separate from Theorem~\ref{thm:pauli}. We do not claim a global review of that article, priority for the observation, or that no correction exists elsewhere.

\paragraph{No unitary-encoding interpretation of the displayed lower.}
Let \(Q_i\) project onto one ladder support block. It commutes with the base and its replacement mixture, but \(\Tr Q_iD=\pm2e\ne0\). For every Hermitian \(H\),
\(\Tr Q_i(-i[H,\sigma])=-i\Tr([\sigma,Q_i]H)=0\).
Thus this tangent is not a unitary-encoding direction even for a global Hamiltonian. This rules out that interpretation of our construction, not all other unitary families or known ancilla advantages.

The NPT implication in Section~\ref{sec:resources} would require an output-QFI lower and an input-QFI upper obtained without assuming the entanglement conclusion. Two observed classical Fisher informations do not supply those two directions of inequality. Full tomography already permits a direct PPT test. No noncircular experimental advantage, fixed-Hamiltonian optimum, capacity or quantum-error-correction protocol is established here. Neither an order between metrics nor local SLD exactness determines a BKM, Umegaki or actual-mutual-information coefficient. For the earlier center-rich rank-two tangent at fixed $\theta$, audit1025 finds the BKM limit
\[
 \lambda^2\frac{1+(d-1)\theta}{1+(d-1)\lambda\theta},
\]
which is strictly below its SLD limit when $0<\lambda<1$ and $\theta>0$. This refutes automatic transfer for this tangent; it is not an upper bound on the optimized BKM coefficient.

\section{Prior work and remaining questions}\label{sec:prior}
The resolvent representation, inverse-quadratic convexity and ordinary unital-qubit coefficient are prior~\cite{HR}. Signed Bloch normal form and local-unitary Choi covariance are prior~\cite{RSW}. The \(2\times3\) PPT criterion used in the separable lower is prior~\cite{HHH}; it is not extended to higher-dimensional bipartitions.
Cao--Lu~\cite{CL} study chi-square contraction and tensorization with stated product-reference hypotheses. Hirche--Rouz\'e--Stilck Fran\c{c}a~\cite{HRF} develop channel orders, generalized contraction and Weyl-covariant comparisons; Section~8.1 also records the full physical depolarizing range. These are relevant prior tools, not new consequences of the present formulas. George--Tomamichel~\cite{GT} give fixed-base and ordinary optimized spectral methods. Neither optimization should be silently identified with a complete equal-reference optimization over arbitrary correlated bases. The unrestricted coefficient of \(\N\otimes\id_R\) can instead be one simply because a direction is stored in \(R\).

Gao--Zhao~\cite{GZ} study relative entropy to a conditional expectation, order/index parameters and index witnesses. Those constructions and the order-comparison machinery are close prior tools. Theorem~\ref{thm:order} concerns two faithful equal-reference states, their individual actual-marginal order budgets and the specified SLD/triangular quantities. A source covering those same quantifiers would affect novelty; selected reading does not certify its absence. Belzig et al.~\cite[Section~4.1, Lemma~4.1]{Reverse} prove the prior ordinary relative-entropy/BKM equivalence; Han--Li~\cite[Section~2.2, Lemma~1]{HanLi} use and attribute it. Their statements must not be repackaged as our discovery. The faithful segment/local method preserves the present convex budget class, but does not compute its optimum. Hirche et al.~\cite{Conditional} further distinguish reference-assisted information quantities.

Ancilla-assisted metrology under depolarization is prior; Chapeau-Blondeau~\cite{CB} considers unitary-phase probe families. The earlier nonunitary ladder is not such a protocol. The different local phase family in Section~\ref{sec:phase} realizes a scoped retention lower, with vanishing absolute information in its limit; no improved experimental protocol is claimed. The bibliographic comparison has been directed, with abstract-only access for one additional2017 metrology source and selected primary passages for the cited2018 work. It is not an exhaustive audit of frontier literature or a worldwide originality certificate.

Theorem~\ref{thm:bkm} supplies lower bounds but no matching general BKM upper. The unrestricted higher-dimensional coefficient, optimal range beyond \(M_d\), minimal sufficient reference dimension, nonunital classification, unrestricted BKM/Umegaki and actual-MI coefficients, tensorization and practical robustness remain open in this manuscript. Proposition~\ref{prop:bell} gives generator-specific equality only in its stated budget range; it is not transferred to the larger rank-two range. The qubit classification and order-budget results belong to one evolving contraction problem; we do not count each corollary or adverse control as a separate paper.
