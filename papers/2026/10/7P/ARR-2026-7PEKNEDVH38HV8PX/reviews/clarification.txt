Clarification accompanying the unchanged reviewed PDF
The optional generalization in Section 7.1 from additive error to a total-variation bound retains independence from the hidden Hamiltonian and preparation/readout phases, or requires an appropriate conditional/joint-law bound. A marginal time-law bound alone does not remove those assumptions. The exact uniform time law is a model resource; a finitely supported clock distribution has TV distance one from a continuous uniform law. The theorem expressly excludes rounding to a finite menu. See the retained referee addendum for an explicit adversarial rounding example.
The 53000-shot example has adjusted null upper bound 0.05577051003, reference lower bound 0.15569441935, and midpoint threshold 0.10573246469. These decimals are descriptive; the supplied rational certificate proves the strict margin and exponent. The verifier's rigorous_numeric_claim label mentions only the earlier shot guarantee; its actual tests and exact-certificate.json include both guarantees.
