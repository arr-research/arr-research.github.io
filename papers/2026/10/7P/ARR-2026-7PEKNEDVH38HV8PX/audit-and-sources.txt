TARGETED PRIMARY LITERATURE AND ATTRIBUTION
Chen et al. (2018), Simulating open quantum systems with Hamiltonian
ensembles and the nonclassicality of the dynamics.
https://arxiv.org/html/1703.09428v3
Read the definition of a common time-independent ensemble and Bochner
compatibility discussion. Existence of static-ensemble constraints is
established background. Our generic finite-time exact alias result is
not inferred from a failure to find those words in this paper.

Chen et al. (2019), Quantifying the nonclassicality of pure dephasing.
https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-019-11502-4
Chen and Chen (2021), Canonical Hamiltonian ensemble representation.
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8113319/
Read/reused the higher-dimensional phase/energy compatibility framework.
Do not confuse lack of a static Hamiltonian law with absence of every
classical stochastic noise model. The present example has a Brownian
phase realization.

Kropf (2020), Protecting quantum coherences from static noise and disorder.
https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevResearch.2.033311
Static noise ensembles and Cauchy-induced exponential decay are known.

J. van der Hoeven, On the computation of limsups, corrected author version
dated 26 February 1996, Section 2.
https://www.texmacs.org/joris/limsup/limsup-corrected.html
The continuous Kronecker torus-density argument is classical. It is proved
again in the manuscript to keep its rational-independence condition exact.

Rockafellar, Convex Analysis (1970), Sections 6 and 17.
Relative-interior and Carathéodory facts are standard; the manuscript gives
the short dense-convex-hull argument it uses. No claim of a new convexity
or finite-cubature theorem is made.

Kato, Ley, Loizidou and Mardia, arXiv:2401.10824v2.
https://arxiv.org/html/2401.10824v2
Read definitions and marginal/Fourier structure of circular copulas.
Poisson kernels and bivariate wrapped-Cauchy models are not new. Their
trivariate construction uses a reciprocal of a sum of cosines; our
explicit sum of pair kernels prescribes all pair-root Fourier sequences
under a sufficient positivity condition. We do not claim that condition
is necessary, nor that our circular joint distribution is a new general
family of copulas. The application is simultaneous static aliasing of all
grid-time channel entries.

Deza and Laurent, Geometry of Cuts and Metrics (1997).
https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-642-04295-9
Cut geometry, separation, and circle-distance L1 representations are
classical. Deza–Grishukhin's Hypermetric two-distance spaces is a close
prior for the two-block geometry of the author's predecessor; that family
is not reproduced or claimed anew in this manuscript.

Schoenberg (1938), Metric spaces and positive definite functions.
https://doi.org/10.1090/S0002-9947-1938-1501980-0
The squared-distance characterization of real completely positive
dephasing is background, not a claimed discovery.

WHAT IS PROPOSED AS NEW
1. Exact finite-support aliases for every strictly off-diagonal-positive
   real dephasing semigroup on every rationally independent finite menu,
   including the proof that its Gaussian correlation matrices lie in the
   interior of the full random-diagonal-unitary correlation body.
2. An explicit bounded-energy simultaneous alias on all integer multiples
   of a sufficiently coarse spacing, by a positive torus density.
3. Complete finite odd-harmonic separation of all rate matrices outside
   CUT, valid for arbitrary static Hermitian ensembles.
4. The dense-menu timing fragility consequence and a bandwidth-dependent
   repair, including a rationally certified 20-time/40500-shot example.
