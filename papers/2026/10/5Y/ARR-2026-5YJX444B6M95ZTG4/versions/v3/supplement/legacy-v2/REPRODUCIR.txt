The illustrative LP uses tolerances and phase reconstruction to numerical
accuracy; it does not certify the continuous minimum. The scripts are intended
to reproduce these examples, not to provide a fully guarded general solver.

All tests use floating-point arithmetic. They search for errors and are not
formal proofs. No directed-rounding intervals or global numerical optimization
certificate are produced. The theoretical grid bound is in exact arithmetic.
The block inequality explicitly relies on Tang et al.'s triangular theorem.
The complementary channel means the full pure Stinespring environment; it may
include a purification of an initially mixed reservoir. State entanglement cost
does not mean channel simulation cost. Axial flat-roof geometry and general
complement transfer are existing results attributed in the manuscript.

The manuscript gives the authoritative proofs. The new theorem concerns the
full complementary channel, parallel LOCC simulation in diamond norm only. It
does not determine the original channel's or adaptive simulation cost.

not a necessary entropy-flatness boundary. The low-coherence plateau criterion
is also sufficient. The non-Bell example uses exact rational parameters:
