ROOT CANDIDATE1096 — complete contraction of classical symmetric channels via a reference simplex
27Oct2026. Exploratory proof candidate, not externally audited, no manuscript grade, no certified priority. Not in frozen23-page1083/publicv4. Binary barrier1092/technical1093 is a different input, not validation of this construction.

Defined problem
On n classical input/output symbols, n>=2, let K_lambda(y|x)=lambda delta_xy+(1-lambda)/n, with -1/(n-1)<=lambda<=1. Its quantum extension dephases A in the specified classical basis, applies the stochastic channel and retains an arbitrary finite quantum reference R. Optimize pairs of faithful states with the SAME reference marginal. This is not a conditional coefficient with unequal marginal subtraction, nor ordinary mutual information/capacity.

Proposed exact result
The complete equal-reference SLD/minimal chi-square, finite triangular Petz and BKM/Umegaki relative-entropy coefficients all equal
 H_n(lambda)=(n-1)lambda^2/[1+(n-2)lambda].
For every normalized nonaffine operator-convex Petz generator H_n is a universal upper. Equality for every individual generator is NOT asserted. The ordinary coefficient for the listed quantities is the prior classical global coefficient
 B_n(lambda)=n lambda^2/[2+(n-2)lambda].
Reference dimension n suffices for the proposed complete suprema. No minimality or faithful maximizer asserted. At lambda0 both are0; no ratio of zeros. Otherwise H/B=(n-1)[2+(n-2)lambda]/[n(1+(n-2)lambda)]. At the negative stochastic endpoint it is n-1: H=1,B=1/(n-1). For n>2 the quantum-reference enhancement is strict for physical lambda other than0,1. Thus the coefficient enhancement over this classical channel family would be unbounded as n grows. Inputs in the lower are CQ and hence separable; this is not an entanglement advantage. The primitive simplex frame and auxiliary reference are prior tools, not inventions claimed here.

Upper mechanism
Input dephasing is data processing and preserves the common reference marginal, so restrict to block-diagonal states. The channel is a mixture of n cyclic shifts with weights w0=[1+(n-1)lambda]/n and wj=(1-lambda)/n for j!=0. The uniform cyclic twirl annihilates each equal-reference difference. The harmonic cancellation lemma already reconstructed in the preceding project therefore gives, for strictly positive weights, 1-n^2/(1/w0+(n-1)/wj)=H_n. It applies to the complex HS resolvent forms and positive quadratic endpoint of every normalized operator-convex Petz divergence, without a bound on R. Local expansion also gives SLD and BKM uppers. At stochastic boundary lambda=-1/(n-1) or1, DPI gives the same upper1. This is use of a prior/project-proved upper tool, not a new theorem of convexity.

Simplex lower geometry
Work in R=C^n. Let u=(1,...,1)^T, P=I-uu*/n, v_i=e_i-u/n, i=1,...,n. Then sum v_i=0, ||v_i||^2=(n-1)/n and sum v_iv_i*=P. Put
 sigma_i=v_iv_i*/(n-1), D_i=uv_i*+v_iu*,
 sigma=diag_i(sigma_i), D=diag_i(D_i).
Trace sigma=1; r=Tr_A sigma=P/(n-1); sum D_i=0. The reference flag u is orthogonal to all v_i. Regularize
 sigma_xi=(1-xi)sigma+xi I_(n^2)/n^2, 0<xi<1.
Its i-th block has eigenvalue alpha=(1-xi)/n+xi/n^2 on v_i and beta=xi/n^2 on the flag and remaining directions. Let r_xi=(1-xi)P/(n-1)+xi I_n/n. The output i-th block has eigenvalue
 alpha'=(1-xi)[1+(n-2)lambda]/[n(n-1)]+xi/n^2
on v_i and the SAME beta on the flag; the other plane eigenvalues are positive in the physical range. N(D)=lambda D. Each D_i acts only between flag and v_i and has offdiagonal magnitude sqrt(n-1). The kernel-kernel component vanishes in the xi0 input limit.

SLD/minimal chi-square
g_sigma_xi(D)=4n(n-1)/(alpha+beta),
g_Nsigma_xi(ND)=4n(n-1)lambda^2/(alpha'+beta).
Thus R_xi=lambda^2(alpha+beta)/(alpha'+beta) tends to H_n as xi decreases to0 at FIXED n,lambda. For every positive xi, rho=sigma_xi+tD is a faithful state for sufficiently small nonzero real t and has exactly the same r_xi. Minimal chi-square is exactly quadratic in t, so this finite ratio is realized without a tangent limit. SLD is the local metric. No singular inverse is evaluated as an ordinary state inverse.

Finite triangular strengthening
Use the symmetric pair rho_plus/minus=sigma_xi +/- tD, with 0<t^2(n-1)<alpha beta. In the active2-plane its blocks have the same diagonal alpha,beta and opposite coherences t sqrt(n-1). The complement uses the full reference identity. The exact triangular divergence per block is 8t^2(n-1)/(alpha+beta), not a normalized postselected divergence. Its output replaces the coherence by lambda t and alpha by alpha'. Consequently its EXACT finite ratio is the same R_xi. The stochastic channel preserves positive definiteness; equivalently lambda^2 alpha<=alpha' throughout this range, with regularization only strengthening positivity. Choosing t sufficiently small for each xi gives faithful finite pairs approaching H_n. Two exact rational examples at n3,xi=1/100,t=1/100 are in FIRST-CONTROL1096.txt and independently reproduced by check1096.py using direct resolvent matrices.

BKM and Umegaki lower
Let ell(a,b)=(log a-log b)/(a-b), extended continuously. The local BKM ratio on D is lambda^2 ell(alpha',beta)/ell(alpha,beta). At fixed n and physical lambda, alpha and alpha' tend to strictly positive constants while beta tends to0; the common logarithmic divergence cancels and this ratio tends to lambda^2 alpha/alpha'=H_n. For finite Umegaki divergence D(rho||sigma), first take t->0 on rho=sigma_xi+tD with xi fixed. The quadratic expansion gives the BKM ratio. Then xi->0 gives the lower. All approximating pairs and outputs are faithful and have the same actual reference marginal. The upper is the Umegaki special case of the harmonic bound. This proves the claimed supremum if the stated analytic steps survive independent technical scrutiny. Interchanging limits or calling singular pairs optimizers is not justified.

Costs and limits
The coefficients compare normalized distinguishability ratios with a quantum-reference denominator. This is not a classical code/capacity increase or ordinary-MI inequality violation. At the zero-diagonal stochastic endpoint, near-unit retention uses near-singular reference preparations. At growing n, one cannot keep a fixed regularization and conclude near-unit retention; R_xi at the negative endpoint equals [n(1-xi)+2xi]/[n(1-xi)+2xi(n-1)^2]. In particular fixed xi>0 gives retention O(1/n), while xi=c/n gives limiting retention1/(1+2c) for fixed c>0. Finite symmetric-pair information and allowed t shrink with regularization; no finite-rate, uniformly robust or experimentally efficient protocol is asserted. Quantitative condition/precision/sample cost or optimal resource frontiers have not yet been derived. The CQ inputs are separable; quantum, generally noncommuting conditional reference blocks are the resource. Binary n2 agrees with the prior ordinary coefficient and suggests no activation, consistent with candidate1092.
