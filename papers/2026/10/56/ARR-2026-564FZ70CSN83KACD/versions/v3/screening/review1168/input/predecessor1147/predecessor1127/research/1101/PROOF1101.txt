27Oct2026. Additional connected candidate for simplex1096, not in published1083/v4. No external audit, manuscript grade or certified priority. Binary technical1093 is terminal; this extension is not part of its input or conclusions.

Purpose and allowed conclusion
Give an actual two-outcome output test whose Umegaki distinguishability, normalized by the quantum relative entropy of a known input hypothesis pair, exceeds the best ordinary classical contraction of the classical exclusion channel. The channel has n>=3 classical symbols, K(j|i)=(1-delta_ij)/(n-1). The benchmark1/(n-1) is the ordinary GLOBAL relative-entropy coefficient, not source-fixed Fisher. The hypothesized benefit uses correlated quantum side information; it is not increased classical capacity, ordinary mutual information, a violation of DPI or a free memoryless-code advantage.

CQ geometry with independent regularizers
Let f=(1,...,1)/sqrt(n) in R=C^n, F=|f><f|, P=I-F and psi_i=sqrt(n/(n-1))(e_i-f/sqrt(n)). These are unit vectors with sum psi_i=0 and sum |psi_i><psi_i|=nP/(n-1). Write Pi_i=|psi_i><psi_i|. For fixed real z>0 and integer n>=3 define
 beta=n^-5, epsilon=n^-(z+4), delta=n^-z,
 alpha=1/n-(n-2)epsilon-beta, c=sqrt(alpha beta(1-delta)).
Define subnormalized blocks of two states
 s_i^+/-=alpha Pi_i+epsilon(P-Pi_i)+beta F +/- c(|psi_i><f|+|f><psi_i|).
Each has trace1/n. The active2x2 block [[alpha,+/-c],[+/-c,beta]] has positive determinant alpha beta delta; inactive modes are epsilon>0. Thus rho^+/-=diag_i(s_i^+/-) are faithful normalized CQ (therefore separable) states. Their reference marginals are exactly equal, since sum psi_i=0:
 r=n[alpha+(n-2)epsilon]P/(n-1)+n beta F.
The classical A marginals are uniform. A phase flip on f of the REFERENCE interchanges the hypotheses while preserving r; the primitive local phase and auxiliary-memory idea are prior, not claimed as a new preparation principle.

Output and full finite divergences
Set lambda=-1/(n-1). The output block is (r-s_j^+/-)/(n-1), with active diagonals
 a'=[alpha+n(n-2)epsilon]/(n-1)^2, beta,
offdiagonal +/-lambda c, and inactive diagonal
 e'=[n alpha+(n^2-3n+1)epsilon]/(n-1)^2>0.
The output states are faithful. Let h_in>l_in and h_out>l_out be eigenvalues of their respective active blocks; g_out=h_out-l_out. The exact Umegaki relative entropies in orientation D(rho^+||rho^-) are
 D_in=4n c^2 (log h_in-log l_in)/(h_in-l_in),
 D_out=4n lambda^2 c^2 (log h_out-log l_out)/(h_out-l_out).
These follow from the2x2 matrix-log divided difference and summing subnormalized blocks. Inactive modes agree between hypotheses; they are not renormalized or discarded in defining the state.

Binary test with classical feedforward
In output label j, measure the rank-one projector onto the smaller eigenvalue of the MINUS active block. With Q_j=Pi_j+F and W_j^-=Q_j s_out,j^- Q_j, this projector is
 E_j=(h_out Q_j-W_j^-)/(h_out-l_out), extended by zero outside Q_j.
The full two-outcome effect is E=diag_j(E_j), complement I_(n^2)-E. It can be implemented by reading the CLASSICAL output label and choosing a projective measurement on R; it requires no entangling measurement between the two systems. It is hypothesis-independent once the known pair/channel parameters are fixed. It is not an operation that learns the unknown hypothesis before choosing a measurement.
The total outcome probabilities are exactly
 q=Tr(E rho_out^-)=n l_out,
 p=Tr(E rho_out^+)=n[l_out+4lambda^2 c^2/g_out].
The complementary probabilities are1-q and1-p on the FULL ambient state. There is no postselection. D_bin=D(Ber(p)||Ber(q))<=D_out. No swap of orientation or replacement of the quantum INPUT denominator is allowed. The labels alone and R alone have identical distributions/states under the hypotheses and cannot distinguish them; the classically controlled test reads correlations.

Fixed-parameter asymptotics (z>0 fixed, n->infinity)
alpha~n^-1, beta=n^-5, epsilon=n^-(z+4), delta=n^-z.
The input eigenvalues satisfy h_in~n^-1,l_in~n^-(z+5). For output,
 a'~n^-3 and
 a' beta-lambda^2 c^2=lambda^2 beta[alpha delta+n(n-2)epsilon].
The epsilon correction is smaller than alpha delta by a factor O(1/n). Therefore h_out~n^-3,l_out~n^-(z+5), and g_out~n^-3. The inactive input eigenvalue epsilon is larger than l_in, so the GLOBAL input condition number is ~n^(z+4). The reference dimension is n and both states are separable.

The log divided differences and exact expressions above imply
 D_in ~4(z+4)log(n)/n^4,
 D_out~4(z+2)log(n)/n^4,
 q~n^-(z+4), p~4n^-4,
 D_bin~4z log(n)/n^4.
For the Bernoulli expression the complement contributes only O(p); the leading log(p/q) is z log n. Hence
 D_out/D_in ->(z+2)/(z+4),
 D_bin/D_in ->z/(z+4),
 D_bin/D_out->z/(z+2).
Any fixed retention target a in (0,1) can be approached in this limiting expression by choosing z=4a/(1-a), but no single exponent is uniform as a->1, and this is not a manuscript assessment target. Fixed integer z values are enough for reproducible rational parameter cases. The noise channel itself changes with n: its ordinary coefficient is1/(n-1)->0. The measured relative gain over that comparator is asymptotic to n z/(z+4), not an absolute positive-rate communication gain.

Limits and falsifiers retained
Absolute input/output information and trace distance vanish: input semidistance is2n c~2/n^2, output semidistance is smaller by n-1. The event p is rare, of order n^-4. Polynomial conditioning in n can be exponential in the number of reference qubits. State preparation, precision, copies/sample costs and the growing classical alphabet are not free; no optimal experimental complexity is proved. An independent regularization epsilon much smaller than beta is needed: substituting uniform noise of the earlier1096 family while retaining these formulas is not justified. Replacing E by a label-blind reference measurement gives zero distinguishing power because r is equal. Changing z with n is not covered by this fixed-z theorem. No all-Petz equality, arbitrary-channel theorem, conditional-MI value or source-fixed matrix-Phi theorem is claimed.
