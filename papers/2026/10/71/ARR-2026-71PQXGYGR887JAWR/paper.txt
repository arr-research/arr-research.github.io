Entropy-budget minimax losses under growing thermal noise
Lluis Eriksson
26 October 2026
Working draft 616, revision 2
Abstract
We establish an extension of the entropy-budget minimax law for oscillator reset
channels to a thermal attenuator in the transmitted branch. The sufficient regime is
environment occupationn= o(E), whereE is the input energy, with the channel fixed
throughout each code. The full Shannon and fixed super-Shannon curves have the same
bounded absolute loss as for an identity or pure-loss signal, relative to the thermal channel’s
own entanglement-assisted benchmark. The key argument combines a bounded Choi form,
a largest-eigenvalue bound for near-capacity inputs, output pinching, and Audenaert’s
established telescopic relative-entropy inequality. It avoids a continuity error proportional
to logE. Explicit input/output truncation errors give the matching common-code bound
without an energy constraint on the reset. A separate extension treats unknown fixed
signal parameters in a known sublinear-temperature envelope. This draft records the
argument for further scrutiny. A working-argument audit supports the stated converse
and unknown-signal extension; it is not a completed exact-PDF assessment or a claim of
priority, importance, formal verification or editorial acceptance.

7 Boundary, evidence and unresolved status
