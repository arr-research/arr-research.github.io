Abstract
An unflagged reset channel transmits through a Gaussian signal channel with probability
λ, and otherwise emits a fixed state whose entropy, but not energy or orientation, is bounded.
We determine the complete high-energy minimax loss curves for unassisted classical
communication, permitting arbitrary entangled block inputs and collective decoding.
Relative toλ times the signal’s own classical capacity, the absolute loss isb(λ,(1 −λ)tα) +
o(1), uniformlyineveryfiniteentropybudget. ForShannonentropy t1 = min{1,B/log 2(E+
1)}; for fixed finiteα> 1, tα = 1 −2−(α−1)B/α. The sharp regime is signal vacuum noise
t= o(E). It covers thermal attenuation and amplification, and additive Gaussian noise
bounded away from zero. A larger unknown fixed family has the same law and a common
encoder/decoder, including cold and noiseless members. No preshared entanglement or
additivity of the reset mixture is assumed. The converse uses causal quantum references,
a summed Gaussian minimum-output-entropy floor and averaged Choi-order deficits.
Selecting a fraction of good positions and using established telescopic relative entropy gives
an error uniform in block length. Finite cq compound coding with exact physical types
supplies the matching operational rate for arbitrary reset energy. A strict flag-loss gap
at bounded signal information proves necessity. The assisted thermal law is also proved,
relative to its own benchmark. The actual critical loss curve and efficient blocklength
bounds remain open here. Working-argument audits support the thermal ingredients; the
enlarged exact manuscript requires its own full assessment.

is directed and does not certify worldwide priority.

quantitative uniform blocklength bounds and arbitrary within-block variation remain outside
this result. Neither (21) nor (22) determines the critical loss curve.
