MATHEMATICAL ASSESSMENT

1. Scope and quantifiers.

The claim is: for each pair m >= 4, s >= 2, an entrywise strictly positive
rational rank-two target exists with at most ten states. The target may vary
with both parameters. Seven states suffice for s >= 9; even m is handled by
six states. The manuscript does not claim one fixed target, optimality of ten,
a useful denominator bound, or the complete connected-source characterization.
These qualifications are substantive and are respected by the proof.

The m=3 case is a clique K_(3s), so the stated within-family characterization
uses the clique theorem correctly. Extending a target by distinct additional
nodes preserves the obstructing Hessian principal submatrix, and keeps rank
two and strict positivity. Thus the statement about exactly ten states is also
sound. The restriction s >= 2 is necessary for the stated occupation argument;
the manuscript does not silently apply it at s=1.

2. Conditioned Hessian and compression, equations (2)-(4), Lemma 3.

The ordered-pair convention, subtraction of diagonal vertex counts, internal
outside edges and factorial normalization are consistent. The identity
(UD)_(ir) = ell*a_i*(1-ell*a_i)^r gives the theta denominator. Coincident
residual labels, including labels equal to the differentiated pin and zero
spins, require care; the combinatorial derivation handles them, and the new
direct-coloring check below verifies every Hessian entry in such a case.

The two limits are in the right order. At ell=-1, the first r active powers
give an invertible generalized Vandermonde matrix. Omitted active powers have
strictly greater exponents, so every normalized omitted column vanishes as
delta tends to zero. Nonsingularity of the selected block makes inertia stable.
Only after fixing a suitable rational delta does ell approach -1 from inside
(-1,1). This avoids an unjustified exchange of limits and avoids claiming that
the zero-entry boundary target itself is the desired positive target. Rational
density supplies the nodes. The condition 0 < ell+1 < delta distinguishes the
pin from the auxiliary nodes; delta < 1/r distinguishes them from zero. The
rank-two representation with independent vectors 1 and a has inertia (1,1,r).

In particular, an arbitrary later minor would not suffice for this state bound.
The manuscript explicitly recognizes this, and its successful blocks are
initial blocks in the relevant active-index order.

3. Ground occupations, Lemma 4 and equations (5)-(7).

The independence number is k for C_(2k+1)[K_s]. Applying the equality case of
Turan's theorem to the complement gives k balanced clique components with
sizes s,...,s,s-1 and the edge minimum (s-1)(ks-2)/2. The construction attains
this bound. Since a clique in a cycle blowup with m >= 5 uses at most two
consecutive base classes, the available 2k+1 classes force precisely the two
listed gap/split possibilities. This remains valid for k=s=2, where one
component is a singleton.

The endpoint correction is legitimate: a strict split has a unique adjacent
occupied-class pair; an unsplit configuration has a unique double gap and
occurs twice in the endpoint construction. Removing one copy leaves the
positive combinatorial set. The binomial weights, rotation factor m, and
formula (6) agree with a separate enumeration of all bounded occupations in
the tested cases. Importantly, empty-clique internal ordered edges are present
in (7); omitting them would change the proof.

4. Large-s symbolic argument, equations (8)-(13), Proposition 5.

The endpoint correction construction is finite and symbolic, not a numerical
fit. The k=2 branch must be separate because its partial component touches both
boundaries; the code and text do treat it separately. The near/far multiplicities
for k >= 3 are consistent with the geometry. For s >= 11 the endpoint lists
cover the exceptions without overlap. Additional ordinary empty classes have
profile 2s and do not affect the five-profile projection.

The six sign certificates were regenerated exactly and matched the archived
coefficient lists, with counts 56,64,80 and 112,196,328, totaling 836. The
central-binomial lower bound B(s)>s^8 for integer s >= 20 follows from the
positive base margin and the stated recurrence inequality. Consequently the
shifted-polynomial certificates apply to the actual binomial value, rather
than merely to an unconstrained formal variable. The signs of Delta_2 through
Delta_5 are -, +, -, -. Starting with the nonsingular 2-by-2 block, these give
inertia (2,3,0); no invalid scalar pivot at the zero first diagonal is used.

5. Bounded s, infinite tails and exceptions, Section 5.

The C9 representative extension in (14) accounts for a new empty class's
outside vertices and internal ordered edges. The k=3 near geometry is valid
with one fewer ordinary gap; the far-B multiplier is then zero. This supports
the polynomial continuation argument without interpolation. Positive shifted
determinant coefficients establish nonsingularity on each real tail. Inertia
then remains constant there; no claim that noninteger k represents a graph
is needed.

The archived table and regenerated certificates cover all 18 sizes 2 <= s <= 19,
all 42 pre-threshold integers, and all 18 k=2 cases. The maximum certified
initial size is eight. It is five for 9 <= s <= 19, which joins the s >= 20
argument without a gap. The state bound is therefore eight plus the pin and
zero states, or five plus these two states for s >= 9. I independently
confirmed the inertias at all 78 tail-base/finite/k=2 points using characteristic
polynomials, rather than the supplied symmetric-pivot implementation.

The preserved failures for smaller blocks demonstrate limitations of those
specific restrictions; they are not lower bounds on the number of spins.
The manuscript's wording correctly makes this distinction.

6. Even cycles and explicit positive witness.

Deleting an edge from C_(2k) leaves a path with the same independence number k.
I checked the relevant proof chain in the supplied six-spin source: stability
excludes split equality occupations, the first four active coordinates give
a block with invertible off-diagonal 2-by-2 block, and the compression yields
six states. Three direct labelled-subset checks, including the smallest even
case C4[K2], confirm the expected initial inertia (2,2,0).

The seven-state C5[K9] witness has the stated rational nodes. The recommended
verifier reconstructs its entire 4335-occupation calculation, including all
edge layers, and recovers the archived scaled integer Hessian exactly. Its
positive normalization is consistent with equation (3). A separate
characteristic-polynomial check of that matrix gives (2,3,0). The trial
ell=-255/256 is retained as a failed restriction, not passed off as a witness.

NOVELTY AND IMPORTANCE

The new contribution relative to the sources checked is the odd-cycle
ground-layer construction and a uniform state bound independent of both cycle
length and clique size. The conditioned identity and initial-profile
compression are inherited, and are acknowledged as such. Odd cycles are
edge-critical for their independence number, so the previous independence-stable
theorem does not include them. The true twins in every replacement clique also
block the earlier no-maximum-degree-twins criterion. I checked the earlier
neighbourhood-gap criterion as a possible shortcut: for these nontrivial
cycle clique blowups the two maxima b(G), c(G) both equal 2, so its strict gap
does not automatically settle this family.

The clique-source theorem and the connected-source conjecture are accurately
attributed to Lee--Oh--Seo and Lee--Seo. Their homomorphism inequalities are
different assertions from universal Lorentzianity of the full source partition
polynomial; I found no contradiction or appropriation of their main result.
Targeted literature searches did not locate a prior theorem giving this same
uniform ten-state odd-cycle-blowup result. That is limited evidence, not an
exhaustive novelty certificate.

This is a meaningful extension of a specific obstruction program, with a
nontrivial uniformity feature and strong exact evidence. Its present reach is
nevertheless specialized: it does not characterize arbitrary connected
sources, optimize the state bound, handle unequal class sizes, or provide
effective uniform parameter bounds. Those limitations explain the moderate
AIRR importance score despite the favorable technical assessment.

MATERIAL OBJECTIONS AND MINOR IMPROVEMENTS

Material mathematical objections: none unresolved. No exact counterexample
was found. The intentional failed restrictions in the supplement are not
counterexamples to the theorem.

M1 (minor, reproducibility hygiene). Executing odd_cycle_ground_kernels.py
directly in the packaged copy fails with FileNotFoundError because its legacy
__main__ expects cycle-ground-classification-checks.json, which is not supplied.
The documented python -B verify_all.py command succeeds, and imports from this
module work, so this does not undermine the stated verification procedure or
the theorem. Mark this module as library-only or remove/document that legacy
entry point. Evidence: evidence/legacy-entrypoint-check.log, exit status 1.
The README already explicitly excludes the analogous historical audit entry
point in previous_own_odd.py; similar clarity here would help.

M2 (optional exposition). Give one fully displayed representative 5-by-5 matrix
or one stripped determinant polynomial in a short appendix, and label the
near/far representative occupations explicitly. The current definition plus
JSON and code is sufficient, but such an example would shorten manual checking
of the bridge from local patterns to the six sign certificates. This is not a
request to replace exact certificates by numerical eigenvalues.

M3 (optional provenance wording). In Section 6, distinguish "independently
written implementation during the earlier source audit" from an independent
reviewer. The final disclosure is already candid, but keeping this distinction
at the point where the evidence is described would prevent a casual reader
from treating earlier and current Astra computations as independent votes.
