RESEARCH DIRECTIONS AND SELECTION

1. Further Weyl rank classifications were considered. No new general
classification was proved, and none is claimed.
2. Ancillary/local variants of stabilization were considered. The
previous stabilization manuscript remains a separate result; no
unsupported extension is inserted here.
3. A simple two-use purity/SWAP witness for renewed noise was rejected
as the central novelty claim because purity testing and state comparison
are established.
4. The selected direction asks for an optimized n-query test of frozen
versus renewed phase noise. It produced the optimized parallel permanent
law, the all-causal unknown-carrier minimax theorem, an exact calibrated
five-query separation, endpoint optima, and a shared asymptotic exponent.
