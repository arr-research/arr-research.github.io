                                               Abstract
         An unﬂagged reset channel transmits its input with probability λ and otherwise replaces
     it by a ﬁxed state. We study its worst entanglement-assisted capacity when the input and re-
     set have separate mean-energy budgets. A uniform estimate reduces the inﬁnite-dimensional
     quantum minimax problem to a classical concave–convex entropy game, with absolute er-
     ror tending to zero independently of the reset budget. For the oscillator, explicit positive
     discrete certiﬁcates solve the ﬁxed energy-ratio limit of that game. The resulting loss curve
     is parametrized by beta and digamma functions. An asymptotically least-favorable reset
     family has an atom at zero below a branch junction, whereas the constructed higher-ratio
     family is diﬀuse. At every ﬁxed interior ratio, the thermal reset is strictly less harmful than
     the minimax reset; the limiting loss has square-root onset and quadratic contact with its
     plateau. At reset-to-input energy ratio at least one the loss is the familiar binary entropy
     h2 (λ). The proof uses global entropy inequalities and feasible distributions, rather than
     an interchange of an unproved continuum limit with optimization. Finite probability-game
     checks support the formulas but do not supply the proof. No eﬃcient coding theorem,
     unknown-channel universal code or exact ﬁnite-energy capacity is asserted.

4    Consequences and scope
The minimax curve is nondecreasing and concave in c. To see this directly, GE is convex in
its reset argument, so its constrained inﬁmum V (E, M ) is convex and nonincreasing in M .
Therefore 2λg(E) − V (E, cE) is concave and nondecreasing; its ﬁnite pointwise limit has the
same properties and is continuous on (0, ∞). It follows by monotonicity that M (E)/E → c > 0
gives the same loss Dλ (c).

    The atomic family is a statement about reset states with diagonal probabilities tL (j) under
rescaling j/E. Its limiting atom does not mean the ﬁnite-state model contains an extra classical
erasure ﬂag. Nor does the construction establish uniqueness of every optimizing noise family,
or an order of physical phase transition. The continuum proﬁles are a means to construct
certiﬁed discrete distributions, not a replacement of the quantum operational model by an
assumed Gaussian approximation.
    Only entanglement-assisted classical capacity is studied here. The single-letter converse
cannot simply be copied to regularized classical, private or quantum capacities. No eﬃcient
encoding implementation, strong converse, or channel-independent universal code is supplied.
The estimates ﬁx λ away from its endpoints in the sense that it does not vary with E.
