# Independent editor onboarding

AIRR needs a named independent editor for other conflicted acceptances and for
appeals. Founder-authored cases follow AIRR-FOUNDER-1.0 in GOVERNANCE.md; optional
independent human review must actually be performed before it is claimed.
The independent-editor role is a human accountability function. A model
assessment, automated check or agent account cannot substitute for that role.

## Eligibility

An editor must be an adult who can understand the manuscript and AIRR's decision
record, use two-factor authentication and disclose any circumstance that could
reasonably affect impartiality. Authorship, recent collaboration, supervision,
close personal or institutional dependence, financial interest and active disputes
are conflicts. The operator records the eligibility basis without publishing
unnecessary personal data.

## Appointment

1. The candidate reads `GOVERNANCE.md`, `REVIEW_PROTOCOL.md`, the deposit terms,
   privacy notice and security guidance.
2. The operator verifies the candidate through an appropriate direct channel and
   records the name, scope, date, conflicts and evidence for the appointment.
3. The operator creates an `independent_editor` account with a unique temporary
   password. The editor changes it, enrolls TOTP and stores recovery codes before
   receiving a case.
4. The operator assigns only cases within the recorded scope. The editor sees no
   unrelated private submissions.
5. The editor reviews the exact SHA-256 version, evidence and objections, then
   signs a reasoned decision. The operator cannot complete that conflicted decision.
6. Removal or recusal is recorded immediately; credentials are disabled and active
   cases are reassigned or remain private.

## Volunteering

Contact AIRR with subject `AIRR independent editor`. Include the fields or subject
areas you can assess, relevant public experience and possible conflicts. Do not send
identity documents or manuscripts by email. Appointment is discretionary and does
not create employment, authority over AIRR generally or permission to publish a
submission outside its assigned workflow.

No founder-authored paper may be represented as independently accepted until a real
eligible editor has completed this process and signed that exact version.
