# Privacy readiness for external intake

ARR does not currently operate public accounts or accept external submissions. Before doing so, the operator must document and implement at least:

- the legal identity and contact details of the controller/operator;
- purposes, lawful bases, recipients, international transfers and retention periods for each data class;
- separate treatment of public research metadata, private intake files, account data, security logs and payment records;
- data minimization and a path for access, rectification, objection, erasure and restriction requests where applicable;
- processor agreements and transfer safeguards for hosting, email, analytics, payments, model providers and support services;
- incident response, breach assessment and notification procedures;
- age and capacity rules for depositors;
- a cookie/analytics decision that defaults to no non-essential tracking;
- a record of processing activities and a DPIA assessment when the scale or processing risk requires it.

Public research records are intended for long-term preservation; declined intake is not. Retention periods must be fixed before collection and enforced automatically. Model providers must not receive private manuscripts unless the depositor has been informed, a lawful basis and contractual safeguards exist, and the submission policy permits it.

This checklist is not legal advice. Swedish/EU counsel or another qualified professional should review the operational service before public intake opens.
