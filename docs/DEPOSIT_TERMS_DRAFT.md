# ARR deposit terms 0.1 — draft

**Status:** operational draft for founder-owned pilot records. External submissions are closed until these terms, privacy information, and applicable Swedish/EU obligations receive appropriate legal review.

A depositor must attest that:

1. they are an author, rights holder, or authorized agent;
2. they have authority to grant the permissions represented in the record;
3. authorship, AI assistance, funding/conflicts, third-party material, and applicable licenses are accurately disclosed;
4. the submitted files may be stored, validated, transformed into accessibility or preservation formats, mirrored, and distributed under the declared licenses;
5. personal data included in the research object is lawful to publish;
6. malicious files, undisclosed executable payloads, unlawful material, and intentionally deceptive provenance are prohibited;
7. ARR may decline, quarantine, label, correct, withdraw, or remove material under its published policies;
8. a deposit fee or donation, if ever introduced, does not purchase acceptance, priority, favourable evaluation, or endorsement.

Depositors retain copyright. Publication licenses are irrevocable to the extent stated by those licenses. Withdrawal stops ARR endorsement of the version but cannot guarantee deletion of lawful copies already distributed by third parties.

Before public intake, ARR still needs a named controller/operator, privacy notice, contact method, retention schedule, complaint procedure, and tax/accounting decision. This draft is not legal advice or a completed contract.
