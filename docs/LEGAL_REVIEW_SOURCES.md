# Primary legal and regulatory sources used for the pilot design

This is an audit trail, not a substitute for advice on ARR's exact facts.

- [GDPR, Regulation (EU) 2016/679](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj):
  Articles 5–6, 13, 25, 30, 32–35 and data-subject rights informed the notice,
  minimization, security, processing record, breach and risk controls.
- [Digital Services Act, Regulation (EU) 2022/2065](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2022/2065/oj/eng):
  Articles 12, 14, 16–17 and the size-dependent rules informed electronic contact,
  content restrictions, notice/action and reasons. ARR does not claim an exemption
  or final legal classification without counsel.
- [Swedish Electronic Commerce Act (2002:562)](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/lag-2002562-om-elektronisk-handel-och-andra_sfs-2002-562/):
  sections 8–13 informed the name/address/email, unambiguous price, correction,
  contract-step, receipt and storable-terms requirements. The missing stable address
  is therefore an explicit launch blocker rather than an invented public home address.
- [IMY: lawful grounds](https://www.imy.se/en/organisations/data-protection/this-applies-accordning-to-gdpr/lawful-grounds-for-personal-data-processing/),
  [processing records](https://www.imy.se/en/organisations/data-protection/this-applies-accordning-to-gdpr/data-controllers-and-data-processors/maintain-a-processing-record/),
  [email security](https://www.imy.se/en/organisations/data-protection/this-applies-accordning-to-gdpr/information-security/security-of-personal-data-in-e-mail/),
  [breach notification](https://www.imy.se/en/organisations/forms-and-e-services/notification-of-a-personal-data-breach/), and
  [individual complaints](https://www.imy.se/en/individuals/forms-and-e-services/file-a-gdpr-complaint/)
  informed the operational procedures.
- [Swedish Post and Telecom Authority: DSA](https://pts.se/internet-och-telefoni/dsa-forordningen---regler-om-digitala-tjanster-for-en-sakrare-onlinemiljo/)
  identifies PTS as Sweden's Digital Services Coordinator and explains that duties
  depend on service type and size.

These sources were checked on 2026-08-30. The production operator must repeat the
review when law, service design, providers, geography or scale changes.
