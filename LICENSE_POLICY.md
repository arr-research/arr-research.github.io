# ARR licensing policy

ARR is a mixed-license repository. A license never crosses the scopes stated below merely because files are stored together.

## Platform software

The software in `scripts/`, `site/`, `.github/workflows/`, and `tests/` is licensed under **GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 or later** (`AGPL-3.0-or-later`). See `LICENSE`.

## Archive documentation

ARR-authored policies, protocols, templates, and documentation are licensed under **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International** (`CC-BY-4.0`), unless a file states otherwise.

## Catalogue metadata

ARR-authored public bibliographic metadata, catalogue exports, and machine-readable schemas in `schema/` are dedicated under **CC0 1.0 Universal** (`CC0-1.0`). This makes indexing, implementation, mirroring, text and data mining, and provider migration possible without permission friction.

## Deposited research objects

Every public record carries its own `LICENSES.json` and `LICENSES/` directory. The default recommendation is:

- manuscript: `CC-BY-4.0`;
- metadata: `CC0-1.0`;
- research code: `Apache-2.0`, or `AGPL-3.0-or-later` when network copyleft is intended;
- data: `CC0-1.0` where the depositor has authority to apply it.

Depositors retain copyright unless they expressly transfer it. ARR receives only the permissions recorded in the applicable deposit terms and licenses. Third-party material remains under its original terms.

## Name and endorsement

Open licenses do not grant trademark rights or permission to imply ARR endorsement. A future trademark policy will govern the ARR name and visual identity.

This policy is operational information, not legal advice. External submissions remain closed until the deposit terms receive appropriate legal review.
